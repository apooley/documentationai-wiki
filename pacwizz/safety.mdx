## Read this before connecting your PacWizz

PacWizz controls gas flow, but it **does not replace the tank's safety equipment**. Check the tank, gas supply, venting, valves, and power before every run.

## Tank pressure and relief

<Callout kind="danger">

**Only connect to a tank with a correctly fitted pressure relief valve (PRV).** The manufacturer specifies an appropriate **15 psi relief valve, or a lower setting where required**. Never exceed the tank's permitted pressure or bypass its relief valve. BrewWizz products are classed as **associated apparatus**, not pressure-rated tanks.

</Callout>

- Maximum PacWizz **head pressure** is **15 psi**.
- Maximum **foot pressure** is **30 psi**, including the pressure from the liquid above the bottom sensor. This is a sensor limit, **not permission to pressurize the tank headspace to 30 psi**.
- The packaging and purge defaults include **15 psi**. A default is not a safe setting for every tank: use values within your tank, fittings, and relief-valve limits. If your tank requires a lower relief setting, confirm an appropriate operating setup with BrewWizz before running it.
- Fit tank-side isolation valves so the tank can be isolated when packaging needs to pause.
- In a foot-sensor packaging setup, keep the **tank-side valve open while pressurizing**. A closed tank-side valve can cause the PRV to trigger.

## Gas releases and ventilation

<Callout kind="danger">

Large releases of **CO₂ or N₂ can displace breathable air and cause asphyxiation**. Purging must only be performed with appropriate ventilation/extraction. Route the vent discharge outside the building using a suitable installation. **Do not leave a tank purging overnight.**

</Callout>

- Fit **CO₂ alarms** in facilities using CO₂, and follow your facility's gas-safety procedures.
- Ensure enclosed areas have suitable vents and extraction. Do not rely on a door or window as your planned purge exhaust route.
- Remember that **nitrogen also displaces oxygen**. A CO₂ alarm is not a substitute for suitable precautions when using N₂.
- The manual identifies specific hazardous thresholds: **CO₂ becomes hazardous when it exceeds 0.5% of breathable air**, and an **N₂-to-O₂ ratio of 84:16%** is hazardous. Treat any sustained gas release as potentially dangerous.
- Plan for the gas volume and duration of the purge; see [Purge Times & Gas Supply](/pacwizz/purge-times).
- Use the recommended [outside-venting arrangement](/pacwizz/purging-connections#recommended-outside-venting-arrangement).

## Noise and trip hazards

Venting can produce **sudden noise up to 100 dBA**. Wear hearing protection when working close to the unit, and warn nearby people before venting.

Route the foot sensor cable, power cable, and hoses away from walkways. A bottom-mounted sensor cable can create a trip hazard and can be damaged if pulled, stepped on, or caught.

## Power and servicing

- Use only a **manufacturer-supplied power supply**. The manual calls for a **CAT II, double-insulated** supply.
- PacWizz power rating is **24 V DC**, **24 W maximum**.
- Arrange servicing through the manufacturer or with assistance from a **BrewWizz representative**. Although the unit is modular and replacement parts may be installed by an end user, contact BrewWizz for guidance before servicing.
- Avoid damage from cleaning/CIP, mishandling, and strained cables or connections. **CIP** means *clean-in-place*; it describes the tank's cleaning system, not an instruction to clean the PacWizz through that circuit.

## Operating environment

| Requirement | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Indoor use |
| Altitude | Up to 2,000 m |
| Temperature | 5–40 °C (41–104 °F) |
| Relative humidity | Maximum 80% up to 31 °C, decreasing linearly to 50% at 40 °C |
| Pollution degree | 2 |

The enclosure is described as a **modified NEMA 4 box**. Do not treat that description as permission to expose the unit to cleaning chemicals or run CIP through it. Ask BrewWizz if you need cleaning or installation guidance.

## Before-you-start checklist

- [ ] Tank has an appropriate, correctly fitted PRV and you know its pressure limits.
- [ ] Gas, regulators, hoses, and connections are suitable for the installation.
- [ ] Ventilation/extraction and CO₂ alarms are in place as appropriate.
- [ ] For purging, exhaust is safely routed outside and someone will supervise the run.
- [ ] Isolation valves are in the correct positions for the selected setup.
- [ ] Sensor, power, and valve cables are connected and routed without trip hazards.
- [ ] Manufacturer-supplied power is available and operating conditions are within limits.
