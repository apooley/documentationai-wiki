## What comes with your PacWizz?

Check your supplied parts before starting. The **remote motorized ball-valve is supplied only with purging versions**.

| Part | Quantity | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PacWizz main enclosure** | 1 | Contains the display, pushbuttons, and quick connections for gas supply and the tank. |
| **Foot Pressure Sensor** | 1 | Measures liquid or gas pressure. Made from 316 stainless steel with a 1½-inch tri-clamp connection. It can be used at the arm or at the tank foot, depending on your packaging setup. |
| **Brite hose** | 1 | A 10 ft, 5/16-inch push-to-connect hose with a stainless steel industrial click-to-connect fitting. |
| **Remote Motorized Ball-valve (RMB)** | 1, purging versions only | Opens the tank's vent path during purging. Has a 1½-inch tri-clamp fitting. |
| **Dual CO₂-in connector** | 1 | Connects to the gas inlet so two regulators can supply the unit. See [Purge Times & Gas Supply](/pacwizz/purge-times). |

The packaging instructions also refer to a supplied **tri-clamp tee (T fitting)**. You will need suitable tank fittings and isolation valves for your chosen connection arrangement; check these before installation. If anything is missing or unfamiliar, [contact BrewWizz](/pacwizz/support-and-warranty).

## The front panel

![PacWizz front panel with a display, buttons labelled Up, Down, and Enter, and bottom connections labelled CO2 IN and Brite.](/images/pacwizz/front-panel.jpg)

*Use the printed button labels, not just their colours, to identify the controls.*

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Up / Down** | Move the selection arrow between modes or change the selected pressure or cycle value. |
| **Enter** | Confirm a selection or value. |
| **Display** | Shows the current mode, setup values, connection warnings, and pressure/time information. |

On **Mode Selection**, move the flashing selection arrow to the option you want, then press **Enter**:

- **Packaging** — manages tank pressure during filling; does not vent.
- **Tank Purge** — automates pressurizing and venting on a purging-equipped unit.

![Mode Selection display offering Tank Purge for automated purging and Packaging for managing head pressure while filling.](/images/pacwizz/mode-selection.webp)

*Reference display photo from the manual. You do not need to open the enclosure to use these controls.*

## Connection names

- **CO₂ IN:** gas supply from your regulator arrangement.
- **Brite:** gas delivery connection to the tank. Its destination changes only for the [assisted manual carbonation method](/pacwizz/manual-carbonation).
- **Foot Pressure Sensor:** provides the pressure measurement used for control.
- **RMB:** the remote motorized ball-valve used to control exhaust during a purge.

Follow the connection guide for your task rather than the product name on a fitting alone:

- [Packaging Connections](/pacwizz/packaging-connections)
- [Purging Connections](/pacwizz/purging-connections)

## A few terms you will see

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Brite tank** | The tank holding your beverage before packaging or transfer. |
| **Headspace** | The gas-filled space above the beverage. |
| **Foot pressure** | Pressure measured at the bottom of the tank: headspace pressure plus pressure from the liquid above the sensor. |
| **Hydrostatic pressure** | The pressure caused by the height of liquid above a measurement point. It decreases as the tank empties. |
| **CIP arm** | The tank's clean-in-place arm, used as a connection point in these setups. |
| **PRV** | Pressure relief valve: the tank's pressure-safety device. |
| **psi** | Pounds per square inch, the pressure unit shown on the PacWizz. |
