## Device offline or not reporting

A FizzWizz appears **Offline** when it has not sent a reading for more than 2 hours, and **Stale** when the gap is 10 to 120 minutes.

The unit may still be carbonating normally — a reporting problem is not necessarily a carbonation problem. But you lose monitoring and alerts, so it is worth fixing promptly.

## Device health states

| State | Last reading received | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Within 10 minutes | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | 10–120 minutes ago | Intermittent or delayed reporting |
| **Offline** | More than 120 minutes ago, or never | Not reporting — needs attention |

<Callout kind="info">

A device showing **Stale** may recover on its own. Wait 10 minutes before investigating further.

</Callout>

## Work through these in order

### 1. Check power

The most common cause is a power interruption.

1. Confirm the FizzWizz is powered on.
2. Check the green light on the power supply — if it is off, see [Power Connection](/setup/power).
3. If power cuts are common on site, fit a [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup).

### 2. Check network connectivity

1. Confirm your network is up and other devices on it are working.
2. Check Wi-Fi signal strength at the FizzWizz's mounting location. Breweries are often poor Wi-Fi environments, and signal can change with tank and equipment positions.
3. If the unit is on the edge of coverage, reposition it or add an access point.
4. See [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account) for the connection procedure.

### 3. Check whether a firmware update is in progress

A device is temporarily unavailable during a firmware update. Wait a few minutes and check again.

### 4. Check whether the device was moved or reinstalled

If the unit was recently relocated or reinstalled, confirm the setup is complete and correct — see [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account).

### 5. Check the account side

<Callout kind="alert">

**The no-organization state cannot be bypassed.** If your account is not assigned to an organisation, no devices appear at all — dashboard or mobile app. This is the most common "no devices showing" cause, and it looks like an offline device.

</Callout>

1. Confirm your account is assigned to an organisation — see [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in).
2. Confirm the device is assigned to that organisation — see [Adding & Claiming a Device](/dashboard/adding-a-device).
3. Confirm your subscription is Active or in Trial.

### 6. Still not reporting

Contact support with your serial number and what you have tried — see [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).

## Distinguishing the causes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Where to go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device never appeared after installation | Network setup or account assignment incomplete | [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account) |
| Was Online, now Offline after a power event | Power interruption | [Power Connection](/setup/power) |
| Intermittent Stale status | Marginal Wi-Fi signal | [Site Requirements](/setup/site-requirements) |
| Offline after being moved | Setup incomplete after relocation | [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account) |
| Offline, and no devices appear anywhere | Account not assigned to an organisation | [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in) |
| Offline briefly, then recovers | Firmware update | Wait, then recheck |

## What happens while offline

The FizzWizz continues to carbonate using its own sensors and calculations — carbonation does not depend on the network. What you lose is:

- Live readings and telemetry charts on the dashboard
- Alert evaluation, so no new alerts are created
- Notification delivery

When the device reconnects, reporting resumes and new readings appear. Because alerts are evaluated when readings arrive, a condition that developed during the outage may not have generated an alert.

<Callout kind="tip">

If a device was offline during a critical part of a carbonation run, review the pressure and temperature trace once it reconnects to confirm the batch stayed on track. See [Device Detail & Charts](/dashboard/device-detail).

</Callout>
