## Troubleshooting overview

This section has several different tools. Picking the right one saves time, so start here rather than at the first page in the sidebar.

## When to run the full Health Check

The [Health Check](/troubleshooting/health-check/overview) is a structured 7-check sequence that isolates problems across the pneumatics, solenoids, sensors, and leaks. It takes about 30 minutes.

Run it when:

- Commissioning a new or relocated FizzWizz
- Performing preventative maintenance during off-season or scheduled service
- After replacing a component (solenoid, transducer, sensor)
- Carbonation results are off-target with no obvious cause
- You suspect a leak, stuck solenoid, or faulty sensor
- Support has asked you for results

<Callout kind="tip">

A completed [Results Form](/troubleshooting/health-check/results-form) with **actual measured values** is the single most useful thing you can provide when contacting support. It eliminates most of the back-and-forth.

</Callout>

## Read this before replacing parts

<Callout kind="alert">

**Fluid inside the FizzWizz is the most common root cause of transducer and solenoid failures.** It happens when the arm separator is not used or is overwhelmed, or when check valves are stuck open or missing.

If several unrelated checks fail, investigate the fluid path **before** replacing individual components. Replacing a failed transducer or solenoid without establishing how fluid got in will simply reproduce the failure. See [Arm Circuit & Separator](/setup/arm-circuit) and the [Pneumatics Schematic](/get-started/pneumatics-schematic).

</Callout>

This single principle explains a large share of FizzWizz failures, and it is the reason several pages in this section ask about separator condition and fluid ingress before recommending parts.

## The Health Check sequence

| Check | What it tests | Idents | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Check 1 | Setup and power supply | — | [Setup & Power Check](/troubleshooting/health-check/setup-and-power-check) |
| Check 2 | Display values on CO₂_In circuit | A–C | [Check 2](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-2-display-values) |
| Check 3 | Solenoid function on CO₂_In | D–J | [Check 3](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-3-solenoid-co2-in) |
| Check 4 | Solenoid function on ARM | K–Q | [Check 4](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-4-solenoid-arm) |
| Check 5 | Display values on ARM circuit | R–T | [Check 5](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-5-arm-display-values) |
| Check 6 | Leak testing | U–V | [Check 6](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-6-leak-testing) |
| Check 7 | Stone hose and isolation valve | W–X | [Check 7](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-7-stone-isolation-valve) |

Every result is identified by a letter — A through X — that appears consistently across the procedure pages, the Results Form, and the symptom guide.
