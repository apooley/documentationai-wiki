## Responding to alerts

The FizzWizz detects conditions that affect carbonation and sends notifications. This page covers what each alert means from the *machine's* side — what is actually happening, and what to do about it.

## How alerts reach you

While the FizzWizz is online, its readings flow to BrewWizz. When a reading points to one of the problems below, BrewWizz sends an email alert to the addresses on your monitoring subscription. SMS alerts are coming soon.

Alerts depend on the unit reporting. An offline FizzWizz keeps carbonating, but no new alerts go out until it reconnects. See [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

## What the FizzWizz alerts on

### Diminishing CO₂ supply

**What it means:** Pressure at the CO₂ inlet is falling. Your supply is running low.

**What to do:**

1. Check the **CO₂ reg psi** reading on the display or in the dashboard.
2. Confirm the regulator setting — it should be 30–40 psi.
3. Change the CO₂ cylinder before it empties.
4. If pressure is low but the supply is full, see Ident A — [By Symptom](/troubleshooting/by-symptom).

<Callout kind="tip">

Running out mid-batch stalls carbonation. The batch then has to be rested with the FizzWizz in carbonation mode so CO₂ can re-dissolve at the right equilibrium pressure. Planning cylinder changes ahead of long runs avoids this.

</Callout>

### Misconnections

**What it means:** Gas is going somewhere it should not — typically a hose connected to the wrong port, or a valve in the wrong position.

**What to do:**

1. Check the stone hose and arm hose are on the correct ports.
2. Confirm the swiffer valve on the separator is closed.
3. Confirm the stone isolation valve is in the expected position.
4. Run [Check 3](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-3-solenoid-co2-in) and [Check 4](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-4-solenoid-arm) to confirm each button operates the expected port.

### Leaking fittings

**What it means:** Gas is escaping somewhere in the circuit.

**What to do:**

1. Spray suspect fittings with soapy water and look for bubbles.
2. **Check the push-to-connect fittings first** — they are the most common source of small leaks.
3. Run [Check 6 — Leak Testing](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-6-leak-testing) to isolate which circuit is leaking.
4. If the tank will not build pressure, see [Carbonation & Brite Pressure](/troubleshooting/carbonation-and-brite-pressure).

### Glycol system issues

**What it means:** Something in the glycol system is affecting the unit or the tank's thermal behaviour. Glycol faults usually show up as temperature instability.

**What to do:**

1. Check the glycol system is running and at the expected setpoint.
2. Look for glycol line runs near the FizzWizz — a dripping glycol line onto the enclosure is a problem.
3. Check for condensation around the unit.
4. Confirm the temperature reading is stable, and if not, run the [Slushy WizzTemp Test](/troubleshooting/temperature-probe) to rule the probe out.

### Carbonation stone inefficiencies

**What it means:** The stone is not diffusing gas as effectively as expected — usually fouling or hydrophobic buildup.

**What to do:**

1. Clean the stone — see [WizzStone-Combo cleaning](/setup/wizzstone-combo#cleaning).
2. Check the flow rate against the [calculator](/setup/wizzstone-combo#setting-the-co-flow-rate).
3. Inspect the stone for damage. Ceramic is not impact-resistant.
4. If cleaning does not help, see [Carbonation & Brite Pressure](/troubleshooting/carbonation-and-brite-pressure).

<Callout kind="alert">

Carbonation stones cause a disproportionate share of carbonation complaints. A regular cleaning schedule prevents hydrophobic buildup and most stone-related issues. The stone **must be cleaned after every use**.

</Callout>

## If an expected alert doesn't arrive

<Callout kind="info">

Check these first:

- The FizzWizz is online and reporting. See [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).
- Your monitoring subscription is active.
- The alert email hasn't been caught by a spam or junk filter.

If all three check out, contact [BrewWizz support](/get-started/support-and-parts) with the unit's serial number and roughly when you expected the alert. To add or change the email addresses alerts go to, contact support too.

</Callout>
