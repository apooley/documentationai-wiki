## What your plan includes

Your BrewWizz plan decides which alert and notification features you can use in the dashboard. If a menu item or setting you expect is missing, it is usually because your plan does not include that feature or your subscription is not currently active.

## Plan features

| Feature | What it gives you | Where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-app alerts** | Alert history and the alert bell in the dashboard header | Alerts |
| **Alert rules** | Create and edit alert thresholds for each device | Device detail page |
| **Email notifications** | Alert emails sent to your account email address | Notifications |
| **SMS notifications** | Alert text messages sent to your verified phone number | Notifications |

Most paid plans include all four. Some plans include in-app alerts only.

## What you see for each subscription status

| Subscription status | What you can use |
| --- | --- |
| **Active or trial** | Every feature included in your plan. |
| **Active, in-app alerts only plan** | Alert history and the alert bell. The **Notifications** menu item is hidden and device alert rules are read-only. |
| **No subscription** | Alert history only. You can't create alert rules or receive alert emails or texts. **Upgrade** appears in the menu and **Notifications** is hidden. |
| **Past due, cancelled, or incomplete** | No alert features. No new alerts are created or sent, **Upgrade** appears in the menu, and **Notifications** is hidden. If a payment is past due, a banner asks you to update your payment method. |

## Why a menu item is missing

<Callout kind="info">

**Notifications** appears only when your plan includes alert rules, email notifications, or SMS notifications. **Upgrade** appears when you don't have an active or trial subscription. See [Billing](/dashboard-web/client/billing) to start or fix a subscription.

</Callout>

The dashboard updates automatically when your plan or subscription changes. You don't need to sign out or refresh the page.

## When your plan changes

<Callout kind="alert">

If your plan is downgraded or your subscription stops being active, any features no longer included are switched off automatically:

- Your alert rules are paused. They are kept, and start working again when your plan includes alert rules.
- Email or SMS notifications that are no longer included are turned off. You can't turn them back on until your plan includes them again.

</Callout>
