## Support and parts

Parts and service for the FizzWizz are handled by Allendale Electronics, the authorized distributor and manufacturer of BrewWizz products.

## Before you get in touch

<Callout kind="tip">

- FizzWizz serial number and name
- Completed [Results Form](/health-check/results-form) with actual measured values — not just pass/fail
- ID letters of any failed checks (`A–X`) — see [Troubleshooting by Symptom](/troubleshooting-guide)
- What you have already tried from the Troubleshooting by Symptom guide
- Whether fluid was found inside the unit
- Whether the arm separator was fitted and functioning

</Callout>

## A completed health check is the single most useful thing you can provide

<Callout kind="info">

A completed results form with actual values lets support diagnose the problem quickly and accurately. If you have not run the Health Check yet, do so before calling — it takes about 30 minutes and eliminates most of the back-and-forth. Start at the [Health Check Overview](/overview).

</Callout>

## Ordering parts

When ordering parts, quote the FizzWizz serial number. Commonly replaced items include:

- CO₂_In and Brite pressure transducers
- CO₂_in, Head_Transfer, and Vent solenoids
- WizzTemp temperature sensor
- Check valves (`CO₂ inlet`, `inline`, `sanitary`)
- Carbonation stones
- Arm separators
