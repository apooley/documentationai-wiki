## Overview

Sign up, create your first recipe, and organize your brews easily with Brew Wizz Docs.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://docs.brewwizz.com/signup`
2. Enter your info and confirm your email
3. Log in
4. Click "New Recipe" to add your first brew
