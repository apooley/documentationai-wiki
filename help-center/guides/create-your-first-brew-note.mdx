## Overview
Track your brewing process by adding brew notes for each recipe.

## Steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Recipe">
    Click any recipe from your dashboard to view details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Brew Note">
    Click the "Add Note" button at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write & Save">
    Enter your brew notes (flavor, time, adjustments) and hit "Save".
  </Step>
</Steps>
