## Download the results form

Download and print the official FizzWizz Health Check Results Form, then fill in actual measured values as you complete each check. Record numbers, not just pass or fail — the values help diagnose borderline or intermittent issues during support calls.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Record the actual measured value, such as `13.277 psi` or `0.45°F`, in every column. Pass or fail alone is not enough. A completed form with real numbers is the single most useful thing you can provide when calling support.
</Callout>

[⬇ Download Results Form (PDF)](/health-check/results-form-v1.03.pdf)

## Notes when filling in the form

The printed form has two known discrepancies with the Health Check procedure. Follow the guidance below when recording your results.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  **Ident U (gloved finger test):** The form records Start − Finish, but the test detects a pressure **increase**. Record **Finish − Start** instead, so a positive value indicates the pressure rise the test is looking for.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  **Ident X (isolation valve closed):** The form says `~10 seconds` for the ball to drop, but the procedure says `~15 seconds`. Allow 15 seconds before recording a failure.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **Before Check 6:** Disconnect CO₂ from ARM and reconnect to CO₂\_In. Allow 2 minutes to settle before testing.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **Before Check 7:** Put FizzWizz in Carbonation mode (not Test Mode).
</Callout>

## When you're done

Once the form is complete, scan it and email it to [support@brewwizz.com](mailto:support@brewwizz.com). Be sure to include your **company name** and **phone number** so the support team can reach you quickly.
