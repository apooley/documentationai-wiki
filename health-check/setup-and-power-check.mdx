## Set up and power on

Set up the FizzWizz for Health Check testing and confirm the power supply is functional before entering Test Mode. This is Check 1 and the starting point for the entire Health Check sequence.

![Pre-test arrangement](/images/setup-pretest-arrangement.png)

*Disconnect both hoses and connect CO₂ to the CO₂_In port before powering on.*

## Step 1: Physical setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power OFF">

  Ensure the FizzWizz power is OFF before disconnecting or reconnecting any hoses.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect stone hose">

  Disconnect the stone hose from the unit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect arm hose">

  Disconnect the arm hose from the unit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect CO₂">

  Connect the CO₂ supply from the regulator to the `CO₂_In` port.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set regulator">

  Set the CO₂ regulator to approximately 30–40 psi.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect temperature sensor">

  Ensure the temperature sensor is connected to the FizzWizz.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check 1: Power supply

Connect the wall-wart power supply to the FizzWizz and to a live outlet. The green power light on the supply should illuminate.

![Power supply green light](/images/power-supply-green-light.png)

*The green light confirms the power supply is functional.*

| Check | Description | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check 1 | Green power light is ON | ___ |

## If the green light is off

<Callout kind="alert">

First confirm the outlet is live and the barrel connector is fully seated in the FizzWizz. If the light remains off, replace the plug-in power supply.

</Callout>
