## System components

A FizzWizz installation is more than the unit itself. This page is the index to every component and accessory in the system: what it does, when you need it, and where its detailed guide lives.

Component names used throughout this documentation are defined here and in the [Glossary](/get-started/glossary).

## The FizzWizz unit

The main enclosure contains the manifold, the three solenoids, the flow controller, the pressure transducers, and the electronics. Gas and hose connections are on the outside; the front panel carries the display and the three control buttons.

| Part number | Description |
| --- | --- |
| FizzWizz-BAE | FizzWizz Bubbles Anywhere Edition, the standard unit |

See [FizzWizz at a Glance](/get-started/fizzwizz-overview) for features and [Specifications](/get-started/specifications) for dimensions.

## Sensors

Sensors are what let the FizzWizz calculate carbonation. The unit reads product temperature and tank pressure, and uses both to work out the finish pressure.

| Component | Purpose | Required? | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WizzTemp temperature sensor** | Reads product temperature, which drives the carbonation calculation | **Yes** — the unit cannot carbonate correctly without it | [Temperature Sensor](/setup/temperature-sensor) |
| **Foot Pressure Sensor (INS039)** | Reads the pressure your packaging line actually sees, eliminating hydrostatic pressure error | Optional — for packaging accuracy | [Foot Pressure Sensor](/setup/foot-pressure-sensor) |
| **WizzKoga Selector** | Switches up to six tank temperature sensors between one or two FizzWizz units | Optional — for multi-tank sites | [WizzKoga Selector](/setup/wizzkoga-selector) |
| **Sensor switch box** | Lets a temperature sensor and a Foot Pressure Sensor stay permanently connected to one port | Optional — needed with the Foot Pressure Sensor on a FizzWizz | [Foot Pressure Sensor](/setup/foot-pressure-sensor#switch-box-option-fizzwizz) |

## Carbonation hardware

The parts that deliver CO₂ into the tank. These are wetted components, so they matter for both carbonation quality and hygiene.

| Component | Purpose | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WizzStone-Combo** | Stainless steel carrier with a thermowell, holding a 16 in ceramic carbonation stone | [WizzStone-Combo](/setup/wizzstone-combo) |
| **Carbonation stone** | The porous ceramic diffusion element. A dirty stone is a leading cause of low carbonation | [WizzStone-Combo](/setup/wizzstone-combo) |
| **Check Valve** | One-way valve preventing product flowing back up the CO₂ line | [Check Valve](/setup/check-valve) |
| **Stone adaptor kit** | Adapts a Zahm & Nagel 24 in stone to the FizzWizz stone circuit | [Check Valve](/setup/check-valve) |
| **Ultrasonic Stone Cleaner** | 10 L ultrasonic tank for deep-cleaning ceramic and stainless stones | [Ultrasonic Stone Cleaner](/setup/ultrasonic-cleaner) |

## Pneumatic and hose components

| Component | Purpose | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stone hose** | 5/16 inch ID NSF tubing from the FizzWizz to the stone assembly | [Stone Circuit](/setup/stone-circuit) |
| **Arm hose** | Connects the ARM port to the tank head | [Arm Circuit & Separator](/setup/arm-circuit) |
| **Separator** | Catches liquid and foam before it reaches the unit. **The single most important protective component** | [Arm Circuit & Separator](/setup/arm-circuit) |
| **Swiffer valve** | Drain valve on the separator | [Arm Circuit & Separator](/setup/arm-circuit) |
| **Push-to-connect fittings** | Quick-release connectors. A common source of small leaks | [Stone Circuit](/setup/stone-circuit) |
| **Jumper hose** | Push-to-connect hose used to move the CO₂ supply between ports during testing | [Health Check Overview](/troubleshooting/health-check/overview) |

## Consumables and spares

Commonly replaced items. Quote your FizzWizz serial number when ordering — see [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).

- CO₂_In and Brite pressure transducers
- CO₂_in, Head_Transfer, and Vent solenoids
- WizzTemp temperature sensor
- Check valves (`CO₂ inlet`, `inline`, `sanitary`)
- Carbonation stones
- Arm separators
- Orange O-rings and hairpin cotter pins (supplied with the WizzStone-Combo)
