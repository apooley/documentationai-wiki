## Welcome to FizzWizz

The FizzWizz Bubbles Anywhere Edition is a carbonation management system for craft breweries. It automates forced carbonation in a brite tank: you set a target carbonation level, and the unit monitors product temperature and tank pressure to calculate and hold the finish pressure needed to reach it.

This documentation covers the FizzWizz hardware, the Health Check and troubleshooting procedures, and the BrewWizz dashboard and mobile app.

## Monitoring from anywhere

The FizzWizz is Wi-Fi enabled. Real-time carbonation and tank data can be viewed from a phone or computer throughout carbonation and packaging, and the recorded data can be used for quality control.

- The [BrewWizz dashboard](/dashboard/overview) runs at [bw-dash.allendale.biz](https://bw-dash.allendale.biz) and covers device health, telemetry charts, alerts, and billing.
- The [mobile app](/mobile-app/overview) brings the same monitoring to your phone. It is currently in development — see the Mobile App section for planned features.

## Getting help

<Callout kind="tip">

Parts and service for the FizzWizz are handled by Allendale Electronics, the authorized distributor and manufacturer of BrewWizz products. See [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) for contact details.

</Callout>

If you are troubleshooting a specific problem, run the [Health Check](/troubleshooting/health-check/overview) first. A completed results form with actual measured values is the single most useful thing you can provide when contacting support.
