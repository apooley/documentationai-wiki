## Mounting the unit

The FizzWizz mounts to a wall, a tank, or another surface. The unit itself is small — 6 × 10.6 × 5.7 in (15.2 × 26.9 × 14.5 cm) — so the real decisions are about access and hose routing rather than space.

<Callout kind="danger">

Power the unit **OFF** before making or breaking any hose or sensor connections. See [Power Connection](/setup/power).

</Callout>

## Choosing a location

| Consideration | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Reach the front panel** | You need clear access to the display and the three buttons for normal operation and for [Test Mode](/troubleshooting/health-check/test-mode) |
| **Reach the flow controller** | The flow controller is on the side of the unit. You adjust it during setup and whenever flow rate needs changing |
| **Hose runs** | The unit must be within reach of both the stone hose run and the arm hose run |
| **Wi-Fi coverage** | The unit reports over Wi-Fi. Test signal at the mounting point before committing |
| **Distance from washdown** | The unit is not waterproof and not designed for direct spray. Mount clear of routine washdown |
| **Ambient conditions** | Avoid locations with heavy condensation, direct heat, or glycol line runs that could drip onto the enclosure |
| **Cable routing** | The power supply and sensor cables should reach without strain |

## Mounting the enclosure

The unit mounts to a wall, a tank, or another flat surface.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the surface is suitable">

  The surface should be flat, rigid, and able to carry the weight of the unit plus the strain of the connected hoses. A tank-mounted installation should use a bracket rated for the tank surface.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Position the unit">

  Position it so the front panel and the side flow controller are both accessible, and so both hoses reach their connections without being pulled taut. Leave enough slack in the arm hose to reach the tank head.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the unit in place">

  Secure the enclosure to the surface. Check that it cannot move when the hoses are connected or when buttons are pressed.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check clearances">

  Confirm you can reach the front panel comfortably, turn the flow controller knob through its full range, and access the CO₂, stone, and arm connections.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mounting the arm separator

The separator is a separate component that sits between the ARM port and the tank head. Mount it so that it hangs or sits **below** the arm hose run where possible, so liquid and foam collect in the separator rather than travelling toward the unit.

<Callout kind="alert">

The separator is the single most important component for protecting the FizzWizz from fluid ingress. Mount it where you can see it and reach the swiffer valve for draining — you will need to check it during [Check 6](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-6-leak-testing) and drain it when it fills.

</Callout>

See [Arm Circuit & Separator](/setup/arm-circuit) for the full installation and maintenance guidance.

## Mounting accessories

Accessories have their own mounting requirements:

| Accessory | Mounting note | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Battery Backup** | Wall-mount within 5 ft of the FizzWizz power input — the output cable is 5 ft. Mounting holes are under the clear lid | [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup) |
| **WizzKoga Selector** | Mount below the FizzWizz unit or units using the holes on each side of the enclosure | [WizzKoga Selector](/setup/wizzkoga-selector) |
| **Foot Pressure Sensor** | Installed inline at the tank foot on a tri-clamp tee, between two isolation valves | [Foot Pressure Sensor](/setup/foot-pressure-sensor) |
| **Ultrasonic Cleaner** | Benchtop unit — needs a drain path for the ball valve | [Ultrasonic Stone Cleaner](/setup/ultrasonic-cleaner) |
