## Power connection

The FizzWizz runs from a plug-in wall-wart power supply. A green light on the supply confirms it is working.

<Callout kind="danger">

Power the unit **OFF** before connecting or disconnecting any hoses, sensors, or the power supply itself. The Health Check procedure is built around this rule.

</Callout>

## Connecting power

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the power switch is off">

  Ensure the FizzWizz power is OFF before connecting anything.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the barrel connector">

  Plug the power supply's barrel connector into the FizzWizz power input. Push it fully home — a barrel connector that is not fully seated is a common cause of an apparent power failure.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Plug into a live outlet">

  Connect the power supply to a live mains outlet.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the green light">

  The green power light on the supply should illuminate.

  ![Power supply green light](https://allenpooley.ca/images/power-supply-green-light.png)

  *The green light confirms the power supply is functional.*

  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the green light is off

<Callout kind="alert">

Work through these in order before assuming the power supply has failed:

1. Confirm the outlet is live — test it with another device.
2. Confirm the barrel connector is **fully seated** in the FizzWizz.
3. If the light is still off, replace the plug-in power supply.

</Callout>

This is Health Check **Check 1**. See [Setup & Power Check](/troubleshooting/health-check/setup-and-power-check) for how it fits into the full sequence, and [By Symptom](/troubleshooting/by-symptom) for the recorded actions if it fails.

## Adding battery backup

On a site where brownouts or short power cuts are common, a [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup) keeps the unit running for approximately 1 hour on a full charge.

The battery backup sits between the power supply and the FizzWizz:

- The FizzWizz power supply plugs into the **power supply connection** on the battery pack.
- The battery pack's **5 ft output cable** plugs into the FizzWizz power input.

While mains power is available, the power supply runs the FizzWizz and trickle charges the battery. A full charge from empty takes approximately 12 hours.

<Callout kind="alert">

The battery backup is intended for **short interruptions only**. It is not a substitute for a reliable mains supply during longer outages, and runtime depends on the operating mode the unit is in.

</Callout>

See [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup) for the full installation procedure.

## What a power interruption does

A brief power loss stops carbonation until power returns. The FizzWizz is not damaged by a short outage, and it resumes operation when power is restored.

A longer outage matters because carbonation is a function of temperature and pressure held over time. If the tank warms or the pressure drops while the unit is offline, the finish pressure required changes — the FizzWizz recalculates when it comes back, but the batch may need longer.

If a device stops reporting after a power event, see [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).
