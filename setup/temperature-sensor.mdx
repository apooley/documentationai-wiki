## Temperature sensor (WizzTemp)

The WizzTemp sensor reads product temperature. Its reading drives the entire carbonation calculation, so where you place it — and whether it is accurate — determines whether the beer hits its target.

<Callout kind="alert">

Carbonation volumes are calculated from temperature. A probe that reads warm causes systematic **under-carbonation**: the FizzWizz thinks the beer is warmer than it is and calculates less CO₂ than needed. See the [Slushy WizzTemp Test](/troubleshooting/temperature-probe) to verify accuracy.

</Callout>

## Connecting the sensor

The FizzWizz detects a connected sensor automatically.

<Callout kind="danger">

Power the FizzWizz **OFF** before connecting or disconnecting sensors.

</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power off">

  Switch the FizzWizz OFF.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to the temperature sensor port">

  Plug the WizzTemp sensor into the temperature sensor connector on the FizzWizz.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the reading">

  Power the unit and confirm the **Temperature** field on the display shows a plausible reading. In [Test Mode](/troubleshooting/health-check/test-mode) the value updates live.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

On a FizzWizz, the temperature sensor and the [Foot Pressure Sensor](/setup/foot-pressure-sensor) share the same connector. If you use both, you need the optional sensor switch box so they can stay permanently connected and be selected with a toggle.

</Callout>

## Positioning the sensor

Where the sensor sits determines what temperature it reads. The carbonation calculation assumes it is reading the **product**, not the tank wall or the head space.

| Location | Suitability |
| --- | --- |
| **WizzStone-Combo thermowell** | Purpose-built for this — the carrier has a built-in thermowell. Recommended |
| A tank thermowell in the product | Suitable, provided it reads product rather than the tank wall |
| Clamped to the outside of the tank | Not recommended — reads ambient and tank wall, not product |
| In the head space | Not suitable — reads gas temperature, not product |

If you are running multiple tanks, a [WizzKoga Selector](/setup/wizzkoga-selector) lets up to six permanently installed sensors be switched between one or two FizzWizz units, so sensors can stay in the tanks.

## Verifying accuracy

The Health Check (Ident C) confirms the temperature reading is *plausible*. To confirm it is **accurate**, run the [Slushy WizzTemp Test](/troubleshooting/temperature-probe) — it compares the probe against a 0 °C ice-water reference, the only way to prove genuine calibration.

| Test | What it proves | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ident C / T | The reading is plausible and has not changed significantly | [Check 2](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-2-display-values) · [Check 5](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-5-arm-display-values) |
| Slushy WizzTemp Test | The probe is accurate against a known reference | [Slushy WizzTemp Test](/troubleshooting/temperature-probe) |

<Callout kind="tip">

Use deionised water instead of tap water for the slushy test. Without dissolved impurities the freezing point is closer to exactly 0 °C, giving a tighter pass/fail band.

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Where to go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature not representative | WizzTemp sensor or internal loom | Ident C — [Check 2](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-2-display-values) |
| Temperature changed significantly between checks | Sensor or loom; confirm with the slushy test | Ident T — [Check 5](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-5-arm-display-values) |
| Slushy test reads high | Usually insufficient agitation, not a faulty probe | [Slushy WizzTemp Test](/troubleshooting/temperature-probe) |
| Slushy test reads impossibly low | Faulty probe or internal loom | [Slushy WizzTemp Test](/troubleshooting/temperature-probe) |
| Carbonation consistently below target with everything else passing | Probe reading warm | [Carbonation & Brite Pressure](/troubleshooting/carbonation-and-brite-pressure) |
