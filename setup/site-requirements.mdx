## Site requirements

Confirm these before starting an installation. Almost every installation problem traces back to something on this page that was missing or undersized at the planning stage.

## CO₂ supply

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Supply pressure | 30–40 psi at the regulator |
| Regulator | Required. A regulator problem shows up as Health Check Ident A or R |
| Connection | To the `CO₂_In` port |
| Capacity | Sized for your batch volume and carbonation target — the [CO₂ Stone flow rate calculator](/setup/wizzstone-combo#setting-the-co-flow-rate) in the BrewWizz app gives the maximum flow rate for your stone |

<Callout kind="tip">

Diminishing CO₂ supply is one of the alerts the FizzWizz raises. On a site where CO₂ runs out mid-batch, carbonation stalls and the batch has to be rested — worth planning cylinder changes ahead of long runs.

</Callout>

## Power

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Supply | Mains power for the wall-wart power supply |
| Outlet | A live outlet within reach of the power supply cable |
| Reliability | The unit tolerates brief interruptions, but a [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup) is recommended where brownouts are common |
| Indicator | The green light on the power supply confirms it is functional |

## Mounting

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Surface | Wall, tank, or another surface |
| Dimensions | 6 × 10.6 × 5.7 in (15.2 × 26.9 × 14.5 cm) — see [Specifications](/get-started/specifications) |
| Access | Leave room to reach the front panel and the flow controller on the side |
| Hose runs | The unit must be within reach of the stone hose and arm hose runs |

## Tank requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Pressure relief valve** | **Required.** Must be fitted and set to 15 psi |
| Stone connection | A port for the carbonation stone, or a tri-clamp tee |
| Arm connection | A connection to the tank head for the arm hose |
| Isolation valves | Recommended on the stone circuit; required either side of a [Foot Pressure Sensor](/setup/foot-pressure-sensor) |
| Temperature probe access | A thermowell or port for the WizzTemp sensor — the WizzStone-Combo carrier includes a built-in thermowell |

<Callout kind="danger">

Only use BrewWizz products with tanks that have a pressure relief valve (PRV) fitted, set to 15 psi. Do not proceed with an installation on a tank without one.

</Callout>

## Network

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Wi-Fi | The FizzWizz is Wi-Fi enabled and needs network access for dashboard reporting |
| Signal | Adequate coverage where the unit is mounted — breweries are often poor Wi-Fi environments |
| Account | A BrewWizz account with the device assigned, for dashboard monitoring |

Without network access the unit still carbonates normally, but you lose remote monitoring and alerts, and the device shows as **Stale** or **Offline** on the dashboard. See [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

## Fluids and environment

| Consideration | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Condensation | Brewery electronics move between cold rooms and a humid brewhouse. The FizzWizz boards are conformally coated, but the electronics are **not** waterproof |
| Splash | The unit is not designed for direct spray or washdown |
| Arm separator | Always fit one. It is the single most important component for protecting the unit from fluid ingress |
| Glycol | Glycol system issues are one of the conditions the FizzWizz alerts on — check glycol runs near the unit |

## Consumables to have on hand

For commissioning and ongoing operation:

- Disposable gloves (used in [Check 6 leak testing](/troubleshooting/health-check/check-6-leak-testing))
- A printed [Results Form](/troubleshooting/health-check/results-form)
- A push-to-connect jumper hose (used to move the CO₂ supply between ports during testing)
- Cleaning chemicals: a caustic cleaner such as PBW, plus sanitiser
- Spare orange O-rings and hairpin cotter pins (supplied with the WizzStone-Combo)

## Checklist

<Callout kind="info">

Work through this before ordering or installing:

- [ ] CO₂ supply available at 30–40 psi with a working regulator
- [ ] Live power outlet within reach of the power supply
- [ ] Mounting surface identified, with access to the front panel and flow controller
- [ ] Tank has a PRV fitted and set to 15 psi
- [ ] Port available for the carbonation stone, and for the arm hose to the tank head
- [ ] Thermowell or port available for the temperature sensor
- [ ] Wi-Fi coverage at the mounting location
- [ ] BrewWizz account ready for device assignment
- [ ] Arm separator on hand
- [ ] Cleaning chemicals and a printed Results Form on hand

</Callout>
