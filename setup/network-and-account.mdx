## Network and account setup

The FizzWizz carbonates whether or not it is online. Connecting it to your network and account is what adds remote monitoring, telemetry history, and alerts.

<Callout kind="info">

Without network access the unit still runs normally. You lose dashboard monitoring and alerts, and the device shows as **Stale** or **Offline** — see [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

</Callout>

## Before you start

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Wi-Fi coverage | At the mounting location. Breweries are often poor Wi-Fi environments — test before you commit to a position |
| Network credentials | SSID and password |
| BrewWizz account | An account for your organisation, with an administrator able to assign the device |
| Serial number | From the FizzWizz label — you need it to claim the device |

## Connecting the unit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Position for signal">

  Confirm adequate Wi-Fi signal where the unit is mounted. If signal is marginal, reposition the unit or add an access point before proceeding — intermittent reporting is much harder to diagnose later.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to your network">

  Configure the FizzWizz for your Wi-Fi network.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the unit is reporting">

  Once connected, readings begin arriving at BrewWizz. The device appears in the dashboard with a health badge — **Online** means it reported within the last 10 minutes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning the device to your account

A FizzWizz must be assigned to an organisation before it appears in your dashboard.

<Callout kind="alert">

**The no-organization state cannot be bypassed.** If your account has not been assigned to an organisation, the dashboard and mobile app will not show any devices or alerts. This is the most common "no devices showing" cause.

</Callout>

See [Adding & Claiming a Device](/dashboard/adding-a-device) for the full procedure, and [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in) for account and organisation details.

## Device health states

Once reporting, the device shows one of three states based on how recently a reading arrived:

| State | Last reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Less than 10 minutes | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | 10–120 minutes | Intermittent or delayed reporting |
| **Offline** | More than 120 minutes, or never | Not reporting — needs attention |

## Setting up alerts

With the device reporting, configure how you want to be notified:

- **[Alerts & Alert Rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules)** — create per-device thresholds and understand cooldowns
- **[Notification Preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences)** — turn on email and SMS delivery, and manage SMS opt-in

Alert features depend on your plan. See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

<Callout kind="tip">

The FizzWizz detects and sends notifications for diminishing CO₂ supply, misconnections, leaking fittings, glycol system issues, and carbonation stone inefficiencies. Setting up notifications at commissioning means you hear about these before they cost you a batch.

</Callout>

## Commissioning is not finished yet

Network setup gets the device online. The installation is not complete until the unit passes a Health Check — see [First Run & Commissioning](/setup/commissioning).
