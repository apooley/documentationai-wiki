## First run and commissioning

This is the first power-on after installation. It covers the pre-flight fixture setup, confirms the unit is functional, and hands off to a full Health Check before the unit goes into service.

## Pre-flight setup

The FizzWizz is commissioned with both hoses disconnected and CO₂ connected to the `CO₂_In` port. This is the arrangement the Health Check expects — you are testing the unit in isolation before introducing it to a live tank.

![Pre-test arrangement](https://allenpooley.ca/images/setup-pretest-arrangement.png)

*Disconnect both hoses and connect CO₂ to the CO₂_In port before powering on.*

<Callout kind="alert">

**The FizzWizz must be powered OFF before disconnecting or reconnecting any hoses.** This is the first rule of the Health Check and applies to commissioning too.

</Callout>

### You will need

<Callout kind="info">

- CO₂ supply with regulator (30–40 psi)
- Wall-wart power supply for the FizzWizz
- Stone hose and carbonation stone
- Arm hose with separator
- Push-to-connect jumper hose
- Disposable glove
- Printed [Results Form](/troubleshooting/health-check/results-form)

</Callout>

### The fixture setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power OFF">

  Ensure the FizzWizz power is OFF.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect the stone hose">

  Disconnect the stone hose from the unit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect the arm hose">

  Disconnect the arm hose from the unit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect CO₂">

  Connect the CO₂ supply from the regulator to the `CO₂_In` port.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the regulator">

  Set the CO₂ regulator to approximately 30–40 psi.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the temperature sensor">

  Ensure the temperature sensor is connected to the FizzWizz.

  </Step>
</Steps>

This is [Check 1 — Setup & Power Check](/troubleshooting/health-check/setup-and-power-check). Work through that page for the power verification step.

## Confirm the power supply

Connect the wall-wart power supply to the FizzWizz and to a live outlet. The green power light on the supply should illuminate.

![Power supply green light](https://allenpooley.ca/images/power-supply-green-light.png)

*The green light confirms the power supply is functional.*

<Callout kind="alert">

If the green light is off, first confirm the outlet is live and the barrel connector is fully seated. If it remains off, replace the plug-in power supply.

</Callout>

## Run a full Health Check

<Callout kind="danger">

**Do not put the unit into service until it passes a Health Check.** The Health Check exists to catch exactly the problems that commissioning can introduce — a misconnected hose, a stuck solenoid, a leak, a reversed valve.

</Callout>

The [Health Check](/troubleshooting/health-check/overview) is a structured 7-check sequence using A–X ident letters. Work through it in order and fill in the [Results Form](/troubleshooting/health-check/results-form) as you go.

| Check | What it confirms |
| --- | --- |
| Check 1 | Setup and power supply |
| Check 2 | Display values on the CO₂_In circuit (A–C) |
| Check 3 | Solenoid function on CO₂_In (D–J) |
| Check 4 | Solenoid function on ARM (K–Q) |
| Check 5 | Display values on the ARM circuit (R–T) |
| Check 6 | Leak testing (U–V) |
| Check 7 | Stone hose and isolation valve (W–X) |

<Callout kind="tip">

Record **actual measured values**, not just pass or fail. The numbers help diagnose borderline or intermittent issues later, and support will ask for them. A completed form with real values is the single most useful thing you can provide when calling support.

</Callout>

## Confirm the installation is complete

Before handing the unit over:

- [ ] Green power light confirmed
- [ ] Full Health Check run and passing
- [ ] Results Form completed with measured values
- [ ] CO₂ supply reconnected to normal operating position
- [ ] Stone hose reconnected and stone installed in the tank
- [ ] Arm hose reconnected **through the separator**
- [ ] Swiffer valve closed
- [ ] Unit reporting to the dashboard with an **Online** health badge
- [ ] Device assigned to your organisation
- [ ] Alert rules and notification preferences configured
- [ ] Flow rate set using the [CO₂ flow rate calculator](/setup/wizzstone-combo#setting-the-co-flow-rate)
