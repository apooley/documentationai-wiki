## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

- A compatible FizzWizz device (check the model number on the bottom).
- A smartphone or tablet with Bluetooth enabled (iOS 14+ or Android 8+).
- A stable internet connection for the initial app download and setup.

<Callout kind="info">

Bluetooth must be enabled on your device. Go to your phone settings and toggle Bluetooth on if it's off.

</Callout>

## Download the BrewWizz App

Download the official BrewWizz app from your device's app store. The app guides you through pairing and brewing.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS" icon="apple">

Visit the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brewwizz/id1234567890) and search for "BrewWizz".

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android" icon="android">

Visit the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brewwizz.app) and search for "BrewWizz".

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">

The app is free to download. Enable notifications during installation for brew completion alerts.

</Callout>

## Pair Your FizzWizz Device

Follow these steps to connect your FizzWizz via Bluetooth.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power On Device" icon="zap">

Turn on your FizzWizz by pressing the power button for 3 seconds. The LED indicator blinks blue.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open App and Scan" icon="bluetooth">

Launch the BrewWizz app. Tap "Pair Device" on the home screen. The app scans for nearby FizzWizz devices.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and Confirm" icon="check-circle">

Select your FizzWizz from the list (named "FizzWizz-XXXX"). Enter the pairing code shown on the device screen (default: 0000).

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete Initial Setup Wizard

Once paired, the app launches a setup wizard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Calibrate Sensors" icon="settings">

Follow on-screen prompts to calibrate pressure and temperature sensors. Place the device on a flat surface.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile Setup" icon="user">

Create your user profile. Add preferences like favorite drink types (soda, beer, etc.).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Firmware Check" icon="download">

The app checks for firmware updates. Install any available updates (takes `<2 minutes`).

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run Your First Carbonation Test

Test the setup with a simple carbonation cycle.

1. Fill the FizzWizz bottle with 500ml of flat liquid (water recommended).
2. Attach the bottle securely.
3. In the app, select "Carbonation Test" from the menu.
4. Choose medium fizz level and start the cycle (takes 2-5 minutes).
5. The app notifies you when complete. Shake gently and enjoy!

<Callout kind="success">

Success! Your first brew is ready. Monitor the app dashboard for real-time stats.

</Callout>
