## Verify the temperature probe

Verify the accuracy of the FizzWizz temperature sensor by testing it against a known `0C / 32F` reference point created with an ice-water slushy mixture.

<Callout kind="alert">

FizzWizz must be in Test Mode before starting. See the [Health Check Procedure Step 2](/health-check/procedure) for instructions on entering Test Mode.

</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the slushy">

  - Fill the entire container with crushed ice, as crushed as possible.
  - Pour in approximately 20% additional tap water.
  - Do not worry if the water overflows, but keep the ice.
  - Fill all gaps between the ice pieces with water.
  - The interface between the ice and the water will be exactly `0C / 32F`.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert the temperature sensor">

  - Place the FizzWizz temperature sensor into the ice-water slushy mixture.
  - Ensure the sensor is surrounded by the ice-water interface.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Stir and observe">

  - Move the sensor constantly in the slushy while you watch the temperature reading on the FizzWizz display.
  - Continue moving the sensor until the reading stabilizes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">

**Expected Results:** With constant movement, the sensor should reach `0.25C / 32.45F` or lower, depending on impurities in the water. It should not be possible to go lower than `-0.16C / 31.71F`. For more accurate testing, use deionized water.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

If the sensor does not reach the expected range, the temperature sensor may be faulty. Refer to [issue C in the Troubleshooting Guide](/troubleshooting-guide) and consider replacing the wizztemp sensor or inspecting the temp sensor internal loom.

</Callout>
