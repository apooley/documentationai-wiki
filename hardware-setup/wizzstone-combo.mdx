The WizzStone-Combo is a stainless steel carrier with a built-in thermowell that holds a 16 in ceramic carbonation stone. Read this page before first use. BrewWizz also has Watch and Learn videos at [brewwizz.com](https://www.brewwizz.com).

## Ceramic carbonation stone

The ceramic stone is designed specifically for the WizzStone-Combo.

| Specification | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 16 in |
| Pore size | 0.5 micron |
| Construction | Thick wall ceramic |
| Tested pressure | Up to 200 psi without rupture or fracture |
| Recommended CO₂ flow | 16 CFH or less on the FizzWizz |

At flow rates of 16 CFH or less, the stone produces very fine bubbles and high CO₂ transfer.

<Callout kind="danger">
Ceramic is **not** resistant to sudden impact. Dropping the stone will damage it. The stone and carrier are round and will roll off a table, so never leave them unattended on a flat surface. Ideally, store the WizzStone-Combo in its own sanitiser bucket.
</Callout>

## Assembly

<Callout kind="danger">
The hairpin cotter pin holds the assembly together. It **must** be fitted every time the WizzStone-Combo is assembled.
</Callout>

Wear gloves whenever you handle the ceramic stone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Insert the stone into the fixed end">
    Keeping the stone level with the carrier, slide it into the fixed end cap. Do not lever the stone up and down. It should bottom out against the fixed end cap, with a gentle twist if needed. When it is fully home, the O-rings are no longer visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fit the loose end cap">
    Slide the loose end cap onto the square section of the carrier, then gently slide it onto the other end of the stone. Do not force it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fit the cotter pin">
    Insert the hairpin cotter pin through the loose end cap to lock the assembly together. Check that it is in place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**If the stone or end cap is tight:**

- Apply a very small amount of the supplied lubricant (about a quarter of a pea) to the O-rings.
- Try rotating the stone in the carrier and fitting it again.

**Pinched O-rings:** an O-ring may pinch or get slightly cut during assembly. Because it is a double O-ring seal, minor damage should not cause a leak. Make a note and replace the damaged O-ring before the next use. Spares are supplied.

## Disassembly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove the cotter pin">
    Pull out the hairpin cotter pin from the loose end cap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push off the end cap">
    Grip the loose end cap between two fingers and push with your thumb to release it from the stone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cleaning

The ceramic stone **must be cleaned after every use**. Its thick wall and small pore size make it very prone to clogging. BrewWizz offers two cleaning methods.

### Torpedo method

The torpedo method uses a tri-clamp housing that lets cleaner be forced through the ceramic stone.

Recommended cycle:

1. Caustic cleaner such as PBW
2. Caustic cleaner such as PBW (second pass)
3. Rinse
4. Sanitiser, or store the stone in a sanitiser bucket after cleaning

### Ultrasonic method

Ultrasonic cleaning gives the best results for keeping ceramic stones clean.

Recommended cycle:

1. 20 to 30 minutes in caustic cleaner such as PBW
2. 20 to 30 minutes in clean water
3. Store in sanitiser

See [Ultrasonic Stone Cleaner](/hardware-setup/ultrasonic-cleaner) for how to use the cleaner.

## Setting the CO₂ flow rate

Use the CO₂ flow rate calculator in the BrewWizz app to find the right flow rate for the stone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calculator">
    In the BrewWizz app, go to **Calculation** and select **CO₂ Stone flow rate calculator**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the stone details">
    Set **Carbonation stone length** to **16** and **Stone porosity** to **Best (0.5 to 0.2 micron)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product details">
    Enter the **height of product over the stone** in inches and the approximate **finish gravity** of the beverage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the flow controller">
    The app shows a **Recommended flow rate in CFH MAX**. Set the flow controller on the side of the FizzWizz to this value. The WizzStone-Combo supports flow rates up to 16 CFH.
  </Step>
</Steps>
