title: Battery Backup
description: Installing and using the FizzWizz battery backup to keep the unit running through brownouts and short power cuts.

The FizzWizz battery backup keeps the FizzWizz powered during brownouts and short-term power cuts. On a full charge it runs the FizzWizz for approximately 1 hour. Actual runtime depends on the operating mode the FizzWizz is in at the time.

<Callout kind="alert">The battery backup is intended for short interruptions only. It is not a substitute for a reliable mains supply during longer outages.</Callout>

## How it works

The battery backup sits between the FizzWizz power supply and the FizzWizz:

- The FizzWizz power supply plugs into the **power supply connection** on the battery pack.
- The battery pack's **5 ft output cable** plugs into the FizzWizz power input.

While mains power is available, the power supply runs the FizzWizz and trickle charges the battery. A full charge from empty takes approximately 12 hours.

The battery pack has three connection points and indicators:

| Part | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Power supply connection | Input from the FizzWizz power supply |
| LED charge level indicator | Shows the battery charge level (only while the power supply is connected) |
| Power to FizzWizz | 5 ft output cable to the FizzWizz |

## Installation

![Battery backup with both cables connected](/images/hardware-setup/battery-backup/battery-backup-open.jpg)

*The battery backup enclosure. The four mounting holes are under the clear lid.*

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a location">
    Mount the battery backup on a wall close to the FizzWizz. The output cable is 5 ft long, so the battery pack must be within reach of the FizzWizz power input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the lid">
    The four mounting holes are under the lid. Remove the lid screws and lift the lid off to access them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mount the enclosure">
    Fasten the enclosure to the wall through the four mounting holes, then refit the lid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the power supply">
    Plug the FizzWizz power supply into the power supply connection on the battery pack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the FizzWizz">
    Plug the battery pack's output cable into the FizzWizz power input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the battery charge">
    Check that the LED charge level indicator lights up. Allow approximately 12 hours for the battery to reach full charge before relying on it for backup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limitations of use

- **Do not submerge.** The unit is splash proof, not waterproof.
- The battery backup must only be serviced by a trained technician.
