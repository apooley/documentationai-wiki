---
title: Solenoid Cleaning & Servicing
description: How to disassemble, clean, and reassemble a FizzWizz BAE solenoid that has been exposed to product.
---

If product has got into a FizzWizz BAE solenoid, the solenoid can be taken apart and cleaned. The process is fiddly, so you may prefer to buy a replacement solenoid from the [BrewWizz online store](https://www.brewwizz.com).

<Callout kind="alert">
The solenoids are factory sealed, and their long-term reliability can't be guaranteed after cleaning. Treat a cleaned solenoid as a temporary fix and plan to replace it.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="danger">
These are dry poppet piston solenoids. Do **not** use any grease or lubricant.
</Callout>

Read through all of these instructions before you start.

## What you need

- #1 Phillips (cross head) screwdriver
- A clean desk or work surface
- Hot soapy water
- A small brush and a clean rag
- Cotton swabs (Q-Tips)
- Paper towel

## Solenoid parts

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Diaphragm assembly | Lower body of the solenoid, holding the rubber diaphragm in a circular recess |
| Rubber diaphragm | Sits in the recess in the diaphragm assembly and forms the seal |
| Piston assembly | Upper body of the solenoid, containing the piston housing and the electronics |
| Piston and spring | Slide into the piston housing, spring end out |
| Larger head screws | Fix the solenoid to the metal mounting plate (not fitted on the Head Transfer solenoid) |
| Smaller head screws | Hold the piston assembly to the diaphragm assembly |

## Disassembly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove the solenoid">
    Remove the solenoid from the FizzWizz and place it on a clean work surface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the mounting plate screws">
    There are four screws on top of the solenoid. The two larger head screws hold the solenoid to the metal plate below it. Remove them if they are present. The Head Transfer solenoid does not have these screws.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Separate the piston and diaphragm assemblies">
    Remove the two smaller head screws, which hold the piston assembly to the diaphragm assembly. Lift the piston assembly off carefully: the piston and its spring are loose and can fall out and roll away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the diaphragm and piston">
    Take out the rubber diaphragm and the piston. Keep the spring somewhere safe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cleaning

<Callout kind="alert">
The piston assembly contains the electronics. Keep water out of the electronics area throughout cleaning, rinsing, and drying.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clean the diaphragm assembly">
    Place the diaphragm assembly in hot soapy water and clean it with a brush.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean the diaphragm, piston, and spring">
    Place the rubber diaphragm and the piston with its spring in hot soapy water and clean them with a rag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean the piston shaft hole">
    Clean inside the piston shaft hole of the piston assembly with a cotton swab and hot soapy water to remove any product. A standard Q-Tip fits the hole.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse everything">
    Rinse all parts thoroughly to remove every trace of soap. Any soap residue will stop the piston actuating correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dry completely">
    Dry all parts with paper towel, then leave them until they are **completely** dry. Any remaining moisture will stop the solenoid working correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reassembly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Seat the diaphragm">
    Place the rubber diaphragm the correct way up in the circular recess of the diaphragm assembly. It must sit centred in the recess. A diaphragm that sits off to one side will not seal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert the piston">
    Slide the piston back into the piston housing with the spring end sticking out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the two assemblies">
    Keep the diaphragm assembly flat on the work surface so the diaphragm stays in its recess. Turn the piston assembly over and quickly mate it to the diaphragm assembly so the piston doesn't fall out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refit the small screws">
    Tighten the two smaller head screws. The threads are plastic, so don't overtighten and strip them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refit the solenoid">
    Refit the solenoid to its metal plate with the larger head screws (if it had them) and reinstall it in the FizzWizz.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="danger">
Run a full [health check](/overview) on the FizzWizz, and make sure it passes, before putting it back into use.
</Callout>

Replacement solenoids are available from BrewWizz. See [Support & Parts](/support-and-parts).
