The BrewWizz ultrasonic cleaner is used to clean BrewWizz ceramic and stainless steel carbonation stones.

<Callout kind="danger">
Do not run the heater or timer when the tank is empty. Always fill to the indented fill line (approximately 10 L) before switching anything on.
</Callout>

## Specifications

| Specification | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Tank capacity | 10 L |
| Footprint (approx.) | 21 × 12 × 6.5 in (53 × 30 × 16.5 cm) |
| Controls | Front-panel heater (temperature) dial and timer dial |
| Drain | Ball valve for quick emptying |

## Cleaning carbonation stones

<Callout kind="alert">
The cleaning and rinse water is hot enough to scald. Wear protective gloves when filling, handling stones, and draining the tank.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill the tank">
    Fill the tank to the indented line (approximately 10 L) with hot water from the hot liquor tank or another hot water supply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add detergent">
    Add about two teaspoons (8 g) of a caustic detergent such as PBW and stir until it dissolves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the temperature">
    Set the heater dial to **65 °C** to keep the water at temperature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Soak the stones">
    Place the stones in the basket in the tank and leave them to soak for a few hours, or overnight if possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the ultrasonic cycle">
    Turn the timer dial to **20 minutes**. Check that the timer dial is moving, which confirms the cycle has started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the water">
    When the cycle finishes, check the water colour. If the stones were very dirty, drain the tank, refill it with fresh hot water and detergent, and run another 20-minute cycle. You can skip the soak on repeat cycles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse">
    Drain the cleaning water through the ball valve and refill the tank with fresh hot water. Turn the timer dial to **20 minutes** and again check that it is moving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reassemble the WizzStone Combo">
    Remove the stones and reassemble the [WizzStone-Combo](/hardware-setup/wizzstone-combo#assembly).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Remember to refit the **retaining pin** when reassembling the WizzStone Combo.
</Callout>

## Limitations of use

- Do not operate the heater or timer when the tank is empty.
- Hot water can scald. Wear protective gloves.
- The cleaner must only be serviced by a trained technician.
