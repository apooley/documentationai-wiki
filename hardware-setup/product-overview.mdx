The FizzWizz Bubbles Anywhere Edition is a carbonation management system for craft breweries. It automates forced carbonation in a brite tank: you set a target carbonation level, and the unit monitors product temperature and tank pressure to calculate and hold the finish pressure needed to reach it. The target is maintained until the product is ready to package.

## Features

### Carbonation management

Select a target carbonation level and the FizzWizz handles the rest. It tracks small changes in product temperature and brite tank pressure, recalculates the required finish pressure as conditions change, and holds the set carbonation level until packaging.

### Operating modes

The unit is controlled with three buttons and a front-panel display. Built-in modes cover:

- Carbonation
- Pre-scrub
- Head pressure
- CO₂ flow control
- Venting

### Packaging support

During kegging, canning, or bottling, the FizzWizz regulates pressure between the tank and the filling system. Head Pressure mode keeps the product at its target carbonation level through to the packaged product.

### Remote monitoring

Real-time carbonation and tank data can be viewed from a phone or computer throughout carbonation and packaging, and the recorded data can be used for quality control. See the [Dashboard - Web](/dashboard-web/overview) and [Mobile Application](/mobile-app/overview) sections.

### Alerts

The FizzWizz detects and sends notifications for:

- Diminishing CO₂ supply
- Misconnections
- Leaking fittings
- Glycol system issues
- Carbonation stone inefficiencies

### Sanitary construction

Ultraviolet anti-microbial lamps keep the lines leading to the carbonation stone inhospitable to microbial growth. Internal tubing is FDA food-grade.

### Moisture protection

Brewery electronics often move between cold rooms and a humid brewhouse, which causes condensation. The FizzWizz circuit boards and sensor boards are sealed with a heat-cured conformal coating that protects critical components from fluid and water vapour. The coating reduces this risk but does not make the electronics waterproof, so keep liquid out of the enclosure and use an arm separator (see the [Pneumatics Schematic](/pneumatics-schematic)).
