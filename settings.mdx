## Bluetooth Pairing

Pair your Brew Wizz device to enable wireless control and data syncing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Bluetooth" icon="bluetooth">
    Go to your phone's settings and turn on Bluetooth. Ensure your Brew Wizz device is powered on and in pairing mode (hold power button for 5 seconds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and Pair" icon="search">
    In the app, navigate to Settings > Devices. Tap "Scan for Devices". Select your Brew Wizz from the list and confirm the pairing code on both screens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Connection" icon="check-circle">
    Return to the dashboard and verify live data like temperature appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Keep devices within 10 meters for stable connection. Interference from other Bluetooth devices may require re-pairing.
</Callout>

## Notification Preferences

Tailor notifications to avoid distractions while staying informed about your brews.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS" icon="apple">
    Open Settings > Notifications > Brew Wizz. Enable alerts for timers and temperature thresholds. Customize sounds and badges here.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android" icon="android">
    Go to Settings > Apps > Brew Wizz > Notifications. Toggle categories like "Brew Complete" and "Low Battery". Set priority levels for critical alerts.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Unit Conversions

Brew Wizz supports seamless switching between unit systems for recipes and measurements.

| Measurement | Imperial | Metric |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Volume     | Gallons, Quarts | Liters, Milliliters |
| Weight     | Pounds, Ounces | Kilograms, Grams |
| Temperature| Fahrenheit | Celsius |

To change units:

1. Open Settings > Preferences > Units.
2. Select "Imperial" or "Metric".
3. All active recipes update automatically.

<Expandable title="Advanced Unit Customization" default-open="false">
  For custom scales, edit the config file:
  
  <CodeGroup tabs="JSON">
  ```json
  {
    "units": {
      "volume": "liters",
      "weight": "grams",
      "temperature": "celsius"
    }
  }
  ```
  </CodeGroup>
  
  Save and restart the app.
</Expandable>

## Firmware Updates

Regular updates improve stability and add features like advanced brewing profiles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check for Updates" icon="refresh-cw">
    In Settings > Device > Firmware, tap "Check Now".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and Install" icon="download-cloud">
    If available, download the update (ensure 50%+ battery). Follow on-screen prompts to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify" icon="check">
    Restart the device and confirm version in settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Bluetooth Won't Connect" default-open="true">
    Forget the device in phone settings, restart both, and re-pair. Update app to latest version.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Notifications Not Working">
    Verify app permissions in phone settings. Disable Do Not Disturb mode during brews.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Units Not Switching">
    Clear app cache via phone settings and relaunch.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
