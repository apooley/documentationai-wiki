## Notifications

The mobile app can alert you when something needs attention. There are two separate notification channels, and they are configured in different places.

<Callout kind="alert">

**Coming Soon** — The BrewWizz mobile app is currently in development. This page describes planned features and may change before release.

</Callout>

## Two kinds of notification

| Channel | What it is | Where it is configured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local notifications** | Notifications shown by your phone, generated by the app itself | In the app, under Settings → Notifications |
| **SMS and email alerts** | Alerts sent by BrewWizz to your phone number or email address | In the app under Settings, or in the [web dashboard](/dashboard/notification-preferences) |

These are independent. Enabling local notifications does not enable SMS or email, and vice versa.

## Enabling local notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">

  Open **Settings** in the app.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">

  Select **Notifications**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable local notifications">

  Tap **Enable local notifications** to request notification permission from your device.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the system prompt">

  Your device will ask for permission. Grant it so the app can show notifications.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### If notifications do not appear

| Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Permission was denied earlier | Open your device's system settings and re-enable notifications for the BrewWizz app |
| Permission was never granted | Repeat the steps above and accept the system prompt |
| You are expecting SMS or email | Those channels are configured separately — see below |

## Configuring SMS and email

Notification destinations for SMS and email are managed in **Settings** in the app, and mirrored in the [web dashboard](/dashboard/notification-preferences). Whichever you use, the settings apply to your account.

| Channel | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | A valid email address on your account, and the email channel turned on |
| **SMS** | A valid phone number saved in your notification destinations, the SMS channel turned on, and SMS opt-in active |

<Callout kind="tip">

SMS opt-in is tied to your **phone number**, not to an individual device. Replying `STOP` affects SMS alerts across all devices on your account.

</Callout>

### SMS keywords

| Keyword | Action |
| --- | --- |
| STOP | Unsubscribe from all BrewWizz SMS notifications |
| START | Re-enable SMS delivery |
| HELP | Receive help information about the service |

If you previously replied `STOP`, reply `START` to any BrewWizz alert text to opt back in.

## Plan requirements

Some notification channels depend on your subscription plan. If a channel is not available, your plan may not include it.

See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan) for the feature matrix, and [Billing & Subscriptions](/dashboard/billing) to start or fix a subscription.

If alert features should be available on your plan but notifications still do not arrive, see [Mobile App Troubleshooting](/mobile-app/troubleshooting) and [Dashboard Troubleshooting](/dashboard/troubleshooting).

</Callout>
