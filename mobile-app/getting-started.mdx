## Install BrewWizz

BrewWizz installs from your browser. Start by opening [dash.brewwizz.com](https://dash.brewwizz.com) on the device you want to install it on.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone & iPad" icon="smartphone">

You need iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later. Push notifications only work once BrewWizz is on your Home Screen, so this step is not optional if you want alerts on your iPhone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open BrewWizz in Safari">

  Go to [dash.brewwizz.com](https://dash.brewwizz.com) in **Safari**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">

  Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow pointing up), scroll down, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open BrewWizz from your Home Screen">

  Close Safari and tap the new **BrewWizz** icon. Sign in. The Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so you sign in again here even if you already signed in in Safari.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on push notifications">

  Tap **Notifications** at the bottom of the screen, then tap **Turn on for this device**. When your iPhone asks, tap **Allow**. See [Push Notifications](/mobile-app/notifications).

  </Step>
</Steps>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android" icon="smartphone">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open BrewWizz in Chrome">

  Go to [dash.brewwizz.com](https://dash.brewwizz.com) in **Chrome** and sign in.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">

  Tap **Notifications** at the bottom of the screen and tap **Install BrewWizz** in the **Install the BrewWizz app** card. If you don't see that card, open Chrome's menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on push notifications">

  Open BrewWizz from its new icon, tap **Notifications**, and tap **Turn on for this device**. Allow notifications when asked.

  </Step>
</Steps>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Computer" icon="monitor">

In **Chrome** or **Edge**, sign in and either click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or click **Install BrewWizz** on the **Notifications** page. Then, under **Push notifications on this device**, click **Turn on for this device**.

In Firefox, or Safari on a Mac, you can turn on push notifications in the browser without installing.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

On the **Notifications** page, the **Install the BrewWizz app** card shows the right steps for the device you are using. It sits inside the **Push notifications on this device** card, so it appears only when your plan includes push. Once BrewWizz is installed and open, the card disappears.

<Callout kind="info">

The installed app works in portrait and landscape. If you rotate a phone, you may get the wider layout with the navigation along the top instead of the bottom tabs. See [Mobile App Overview](/mobile-app/overview#getting-around).

</Callout>

## Signing in

Use the same email address and password as the dashboard. There is no way to create an account in the app. BrewWizz sends each person an invitation; see [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in).

1. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
2. Tap **Sign in**. BrewWizz opens on your **Devices** screen.

You stay signed in until you sign out or your sign-in expires, even when you close the app. If your sign-in expires, BrewWizz shows **Your session expired. Please sign in again.**

If you tapped **Acknowledge** on a notification while signed out, BrewWizz asks you to sign in first and then takes you straight to that alert. See [Alerts](/mobile-app/alerts#acknowledging-from-a-notification).

## Forgot password

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address and tap **Send reset link**. You always see the same confirmation, whether or not the address has an account.
3. Open the email and tap the link. It opens in your browser, not in the installed app.
4. Choose a new password of at least 8 characters on the **Set your password** page and tap **Save password**.
5. Go back to the BrewWizz app and sign in with your new password.

## If BrewWizz shows only Billing

Your organization does not have an active BrewWizz subscription. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing).
