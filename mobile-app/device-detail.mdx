## Open a device

Tap a card on the **Devices** screen, or tap a push notification about a device, to open its detail screen. Everything from the desktop [device page](/dashboard/device-detail) is here, laid out to fit your screen.

- **On a phone**, everything stacks into one scrolling screen.
- **On a tablet** (about 768 pixels wide and up), the screen splits in two. A narrow column on the left holds the device and batch cards, and the latest reading, tabs and charts fill the rest.

## What's on the screen

From top to bottom on a phone:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | **Back to devices**, the device name, **Updated … ago**, **Live** (only while the device is online), and the health state |
| **Gauges** | Temperature, Carbonation, CO₂ supply and Tank pressure. They dim with **Offline · showing values … ago** when the device is not reporting. |
| **Summary cards** | Expected carbonation (T+P), Pressure control, Carbonation target and the carbonation and tank pressure rates |
| **Device card** | Rename with the pencil, serial number, firmware, network, last reading, and **Display units** |
| **Batch card** | The current batch, its name, start date and start temperature, its carbonation target, and a link to the batch report |
| **Notes** | Notes for the batch. Add one with **Add a note** and **Add note**. |
| **Latest reading** | The most recent values from the unit |
| **Tabs** | **Charts**, **Readings**, **Batches** and **Alerts** |

<Callout kind="tip">

To answer "what is it doing right now?", the gauges and the Pressure control card at the top are the quickest check. Scroll down to the charts for history.

</Callout>

## Staying up to date

New readings appear on the gauges and the latest reading by themselves while the screen is open. If you leave BrewWizz for 30 seconds or more and come back, or your connection returns, the screen reloads its data on its own, so you never need to pull down to refresh. The **Updated … ago** line under the device name shows how fresh it is.

## Charts on a phone

- Pick a time range: **1h**, **24h**, **7d**, **30d**, **This batch** or **Custom**. **This batch** is greyed out until the device has recorded a batch. **Custom** asks for a start and end time and an **Apply** tap.
- Switch the chart style with the line, area and bar buttons, or tap **All Metrics** to overlay everything on one chart.
- Drag the handles on the zoom bar above the charts to look closer at part of the range, and tap **Reset zoom** to go back.
- **Export CSV** works on a phone, but large exports are easier on a computer.

### Full-screen charts

Every chart has an **expand** button in its top-right corner. Tap it to open the chart full screen. It fills the whole display in portrait and landscape, so turn your phone sideways for the widest view. Tap **Close** (or press Esc on a keyboard) to go back to the page.

## Readings as cards

On the **Readings** tab, a phone shows each reading as its own card instead of a wide table. A card shows the time, **Carbonation**, **Tank P** (with its target), **Temp** and **CO₂ supply**, plus any flags. There is no sideways swiping. Use the **Prev** and **Next** buttons and the **Rows** menu at the bottom to move through older readings.

A tablet shows cards too when the screen is narrow, and the full table (with **Time**, **Temp**, **Tank P**, **Target P**, **CO₂**, **Carbonation** and **Flags** columns) when there is room for it.

For what each chart and value means, see [Device Detail & Charts](/dashboard/device-detail) and [Understanding Your Readings](/dashboard/readings-explained).

## Notes from the brewhouse floor

The phone is the handiest place to add batch notes, such as a cylinder change, a dry-hop addition or a tasting result, while you are at the tank. Everyone in your organization sees them on the same batch.

## Alert rules

On the **Alerts** tab, tap **Add Rule**, choose a **Metric**, **above** or **below**, and a **Threshold** in your display units, then tap **Create**. Use a rule's switch to pause it, or its delete button to remove it (BrewWizz asks you to confirm). See [Alerts & Alert Rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules).
