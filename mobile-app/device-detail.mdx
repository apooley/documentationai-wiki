## Open a device

Tap a card on the **Devices** screen, or tap a push notification about a device, to open its detail screen. Everything from the desktop [device page](/dashboard/device-detail) is here, stacked into one scrolling screen.

## What's on the screen

From top to bottom:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | **Back to devices**, the device name, **Live**, and the health state |
| **Gauges** | Temperature, Carbonation, CO₂ supply and Tank pressure. They dim with **Offline · showing values … ago** when the device is not reporting. |
| **Summary cards** | Expected carbonation (T+P), Pressure control, Style band and rates |
| **Device card** | Rename with the pencil, serial number, firmware, network, last reading, and **Display units** |
| **Batch card** | The current batch, its name, start time and start temperature |
| **Notes** | Notes for the batch. Add one with **Add a note** and **Add note**. |
| **Latest reading** | The most recent values from the unit |
| **Tabs** | **Charts**, **Readings**, **Batches** and **Alerts** |

<Callout kind="tip">

To answer "what is it doing right now?", the gauges and the Pressure control card at the top are the quickest check. Scroll down to the charts for history.

</Callout>

## Charts and readings on a phone

- Pick a time range: **1h**, **24h**, **7d**, **30d**, **This batch** or **Custom**. The 1h and 24h views update live.
- Drag the zoom bar under the charts to look closer at part of the range, and tap **Reset zoom** to go back.
- The **Readings** table is wider than a phone screen, so swipe sideways to see every column, or turn your phone to landscape.
- **Export CSV** works on a phone, but large exports are easier on a computer.

For what each chart and value means, see [Device Detail & Charts](/dashboard/device-detail) and [Understanding Your Readings](/dashboard/readings-explained).

## Notes from the brewhouse floor

The phone is the handiest place to add batch notes, such as a cylinder change, a dry-hop addition or a tasting result, while you are at the tank. Everyone in your organization sees them on the same batch.

## Alert rules

On the **Alerts** tab, tap **+ Add Rule** and choose a metric, **above** or **below**, and a threshold in your display units, then tap **Create**. Use a rule's switch to pause it, or its delete button to remove it. See [Alerts & Alert Rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules).
