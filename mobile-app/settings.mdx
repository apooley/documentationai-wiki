## The menu

BrewWizz has no separate Settings screen. Everything is in the menu (☰) at the top right of every screen: your pages, your unit selectors, **Help** and **Sign out**.

## Display units

Use the unit selectors in the menu to switch temperature (°C or °F), pressure (bar or psi) and carbonation (g/L or volumes). The same selectors are under **Display units** on each device screen. For one-tap **Metric** or **US** presets, go to **Notifications**.

Units are saved to your account, so your phone, computer and alert messages all use the same ones. See [Display units](/dashboard/overview#display-units).

## Billing

**Billing** shows your plan, status, renewal date and device allowance. Tap **Manage subscription** to open the billing portal in your browser, where you can update your payment method or change your plan. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing).

If your organization's subscription is not active, BrewWizz shows only the Billing screen until it is renewed.

## Signing out

Tap **Sign out** in the menu. Signing out also turns off push notifications on that phone or computer. Turn push back on after you sign in again if you still want it there.

## Updates

BrewWizz updates itself. When you open the app with a connection, it downloads the new version in the background. The update takes effect the next time you open the app. To get it straight away, close BrewWizz completely and open it again.

## Removing the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove push from this device first">

  Go to **Notifications** and remove the **This device** push channel, or just tap **Sign out**. This stops BrewWizz sending alerts to a phone that no longer has the app.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the icon">

  Remove BrewWizz like any other app. On iPhone, press and hold the icon and choose **Remove App**. On Android, press and hold and choose **Uninstall**. On a computer, open BrewWizz and choose **Uninstall** from its window menu.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing the app does not affect your account, your devices or your other phones and computers.

## Help

**Help** in the menu opens this help center in your browser. For anything else, see [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).
