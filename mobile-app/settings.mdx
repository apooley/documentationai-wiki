## The More menu

BrewWizz has no separate Settings screen. On a phone, tap **More** at the right end of the bottom tab bar to open a sheet that holds:

- **API**, if your plan includes it
- **Billing**
- **Help**
- your unit selectors (temperature, pressure, carbonation)
- your name and **Sign out**

Tap anywhere outside the sheet, or tap a link in it, to close it. On a tablet, computer, or a phone held sideways, the same items are in the bar along the top instead.

## Display units

Use the unit selectors in the More sheet to switch temperature (°C or °F), pressure (bar or psi) and carbonation (g/L or volumes). The change saves as soon as you pick it, and the selectors are greyed out while you are offline. The same selectors are under **Display units** on each device screen. For one-tap **Metric** or **US** presets, go to **Notifications**, pick a preset and tap **Save units**. If you have set your own units, **Use org default** there goes back to your organization's choice.

Units are saved to your account, so your phone, computer and alert messages all use the same ones. See [Display units](/dashboard/overview#display-units).

## Billing

**Billing** shows your plan, status, renewal date and device allowance. Tap **Manage subscription** to open the billing portal in your browser, where you can update your payment method or change your plan. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing).

If your organization's subscription is not active, BrewWizz shows only the Billing screen until it is renewed.

## Signing out

Tap **More**, then **Sign out**. Signing out also turns off push notifications on that phone or computer. Turn push back on after you sign in again if you still want it there. If your sign-in simply expires, push is not turned off.

## Updates

BrewWizz updates itself. When you open the app with a connection, it downloads the new version in the background. You don't need to do anything, and there is no update button. The new version takes over the next time BrewWizz starts, so if something looks out of date, close BrewWizz completely and open it again.

## Removing the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove push from this device first">

  Go to **Notifications** and remove the **This device** push channel, or just tap **Sign out**. This stops BrewWizz sending alerts to a phone that no longer has the app.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the icon">

  Remove BrewWizz like any other app. On iPhone, press and hold the icon and choose **Remove App**. On Android, press and hold and choose **Uninstall**. On a computer, open BrewWizz and choose **Uninstall** from its window menu.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing the app does not affect your account, your devices or your other phones and computers.

## Help

**Help** in the More sheet opens this help center in your browser. For anything else, see [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).
