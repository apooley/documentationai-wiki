## The BrewWizz mobile app at a glance

The BrewWizz mobile app is the [BrewWizz dashboard](/dashboard/overview), installed straight from your browser. There is nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play. It uses the same account and shows the same devices, alerts and settings, so anything you change on your phone shows up on your computer, and the other way round.

Installing BrewWizz gives you:

- **Its own icon.** BrewWizz opens full screen, like any other app.
- **Push notifications.** Alerts, and notices that a device has stopped reporting or a batch is ready, appear on your phone even when BrewWizz is closed.
- **Automatic updates.** New versions arrive by themselves, with no app store involved.

<Callout kind="info">

On iPhone and iPad, push notifications only work after you add BrewWizz to your Home Screen. On Android and computers you can turn on push in the browser without installing, but installing is still recommended.

</Callout>

## What it needs

| Device | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **iPhone or iPad** | iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later, installed from Safari |
| **Android** | Chrome |
| **Computer** | Chrome or Edge to install. Push also works in Firefox, and in Safari on a Mac, without installing. |

You also need an internet connection. BrewWizz shows live data, so it does not work offline. See [Offline](#offline).

## Getting around

On a phone held upright, BrewWizz has a **bar of tabs along the bottom** of the screen:

| Tab | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Devices** | Your [device list](/mobile-app/devices) |
| **Alerts** | The [Alerts screen](/mobile-app/alerts) |
| **Notifications** | [Push](/mobile-app/notifications), email and SMS preferences, display units and the weekly email |
| **More** | **API** (if your plan includes it), **Billing**, **Help**, your unit selectors and **Sign out** |

The **alert bell**, with its red count of unacknowledged alerts, sits at the top of every screen.

Which tabs you see depends on your plan. **Alerts** and **Notifications** only appear if your plan includes them, and if your plan needs upgrading a single **Upgrade** tab takes their place. See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

On a tablet, a computer, or a phone held sideways (anything wider than about 640 pixels), the tabs move to a bar along the top with the same pages, plus **Billing**, **Help**, the unit selectors and a sign-out button.

## Device health states

The same health states appear on every screen, on your phone and on the desktop:

| State | Colour | Last reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Green | Within 10 minutes | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | Yellow | 10–120 minutes ago | Reporting intermittently |
| **Offline** | Red | More than 120 minutes ago, or never | Not reporting; needs attention |

## Staying up to date

While BrewWizz is open, readings, health states and new alerts appear by themselves without you refreshing. The Devices screen, each device screen and the tank board show a quiet **Updated 2 min ago** line near the top so you can see how fresh the data is.

When you switch away and come back, BrewWizz catches up for you. If you were away for 30 seconds or more, or your connection returns, it reloads the data on its own when you reopen it. You never need to pull down to refresh.

When BrewWizz is closed, [push notifications](/mobile-app/notifications) tell you about alerts.

## Made for a bigger screen, too

- **Readings as cards.** On a phone, the **Readings** tab on a device shows each reading as a compact card instead of a wide table. See [Device Detail](/mobile-app/device-detail).
- **Full-screen charts.** Every chart has an expand button that opens it full screen, in portrait or landscape.
- **Two columns on a tablet.** From tablet width, the device screen splits into an information column and a charts column.
- **Tank board and Cellar screen.** The **Board view** link on the Devices screen opens a large-tile view of all your tanks. **Cellar screen** turns it into a wall display that keeps the screen awake. See [Device List](/mobile-app/devices#board-view-and-cellar-screen).

## Offline

If your phone loses its connection, an amber bar reads **You are offline — data may be outdated**. The screen keeps showing what was already loaded, but buttons that save changes are greyed out until you reconnect. Readings are not stored on your phone, so opening BrewWizz without a connection shows no device data.
