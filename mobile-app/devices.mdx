## Your device list

**Devices** is the first screen you see in BrewWizz. Each FizzWizz in your organization appears as a card, one under another, with devices that need attention at the top. Tap a card to open the [device's detail screen](/mobile-app/device-detail).

## What each card shows

| Element | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The device name, or its serial number if it has no name, with the serial number and firmware version underneath |
| **Health pill** | Online, Stale or Offline |
| **Gauges** | Temperature, Tank pressure and CO₂ supply |
| **Carbonation** | The current carbonation, and **Δ pressure**, the gap between tank and target pressure |
| **Chips** | Pressure control (**On**, **Near** or **Off setpoint**) and, if a style band is set, **Below target**, **In target band** or **Above target** |
| **Last reading** | How long ago the device last reported, or **Never** |

A red border means the device is Offline. An amber border means it is Stale, off its target pressure, or outside its style band. For the details, see [Devices & Health States](/dashboard/devices).

## Health states

| State | Colour | Last reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Green | Within 10 minutes |
| **Stale** | Yellow | 10–120 minutes ago |
| **Offline** | Red | More than 120 minutes ago, or never |

## Live updates

The list updates by itself while BrewWizz is open. A card moves up or down as soon as its state changes, so you don't need to refresh.

## Claiming a device from your phone

The **Claim a device** card is also on this screen. That makes it easy to claim a unit while you are standing at the tank: enter the 6-digit code from the FizzWizz display in **Claim code** and tap **Claim device**. See [Adding & Claiming a Device](/dashboard/adding-a-device).

<Callout kind="alert">

Each card has a **Release device** button. BrewWizz asks you to confirm before it removes the device from your organization. If you release one by mistake, claim it again with the new code on its display.

</Callout>

## When the list is empty

**No devices yet** means no devices are claimed for your organization yet. Claim one with the code on its display, or contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) to have one assigned.
