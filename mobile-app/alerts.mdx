## Alerts on your phone

When an [alert rule](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules) fires, the alert shows up in three places on your phone:

- **The alert bell**, at the top of every screen
- **The Alerts screen**, in the menu (☰)
- **A push notification**, if you have [turned push on](/mobile-app/notifications) for this phone, even when BrewWizz is closed

<Callout kind="info">

The bell and the Alerts screen need **in-app alerts** on your plan, and push needs **push notifications**. If **Alerts** isn't in the menu, see [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

</Callout>

## The alert bell

The red badge on the bell shows how many alerts nobody has acknowledged yet, up to **99+**. It updates live.

Tap the bell to see the five most recent unacknowledged alerts. Tap **✓** next to one to acknowledge it, or **View all alerts** to open the Alerts screen. **No unacknowledged alerts** means you are all caught up.

## The Alerts screen

- **Active**, the default, shows alerts that haven't been acknowledged. **All** shows the full history, up to the latest 100.
- Each alert shows its severity (**Warning** or **Critical**), the device, the reading compared with the rule's threshold, and when it fired.
- Tap **Acknowledge** to mark an alert as dealt with. Acknowledged alerts stay in the history.

When there is nothing to show, the screen reads **All clear! No active alerts.** or **No alerts have been triggered yet.**

## Push notifications

A push notification names the device, the reading and the threshold, in your display units. Tap it to open that device's detail screen, so you can check its gauges and charts straight away.

If you have turned on **Also tell me when a device stops reporting for 2 hours** for push, you also get a **Device stopped reporting** notification when a unit goes quiet. These notices don't appear on the Alerts screen or in the bell.
