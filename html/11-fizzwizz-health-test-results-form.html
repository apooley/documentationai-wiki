This is the record sheet for a full FizzWizz Health Check, with every criterion in one place. Use it as your working reference while testing, and keep the completed record with the unit.

Record actual measured values, not just pass or fail. A reading that passes today but has moved since the last check is early warning of a developing fault, and it is the first thing support will ask you for.
