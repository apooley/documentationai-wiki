Before you get in touch

Having these to hand will get you to an answer considerably faster:

  • The FizzWizz serial number and the unit's name, if you have named it
  • A completed Health Test Results Form — with actual measured values, not just pass or fail
  • The ID letters of any checks that failed (A–X)
  • What you have already tried from Troubleshooting by Symptom
  • Whether any fluid was found inside the unit, and whether the arm separator was fitted and functioning

A completed health check is the single most useful thing you can provide. It turns an open-ended diagnostic conversation into a specific one.

Ordering parts

Commonly replaced items include the CO2_In and Brite pressure transducers, the CO2_in, Head_Transfer and Vent solenoids, the WizzTemp temperature sensor, check valves, carbonation stones and the arm separator.

Quote the serial number when ordering so the correct revision is supplied.
