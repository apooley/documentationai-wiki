Check 3 proves two things: that no solenoid is leaking when at rest, and that each button operates the solenoid it should.

Preconditions

  • CO2 connected to CO2_In, regulator at 30–40 psi
  • Stone hose and arm hose disconnected, so you can feel and hear flow at each port
  • FizzWizz in Test Mode
  • Check 2 passed

Button to solenoid mapping

ButtonSolenoid operated
UpHead_Transfer
DownCO2_in
EnterVent

Check 3.1: at rest, nothing should flow

With CO2 connected and no buttons pressed, confirm there is no gas flowing from any of the following:

IDConfirm no flow fromPass
DStone connectionNo flow
EArm connectionNo flow
FVent valveNo flow
GFlow meter ball should be downBall down, no flow

Any flow at rest means a solenoid is stuck open.

Checks 3.2 to 3.4: each button pulses the right port

Press and hold each button in turn:

IDPress and holdExpected result
HUpGas pulses out of the Arm connection
IDownGas pulses out of the Stone connection, and the flow meter ball bounces
JEnterNo gas flows from anywhere — you should hear a quiet click

Check J is a listening test, not a flow test. With CO2 on the CO2_In port, operating the vent solenoid should not release gas; the click confirms the solenoid is actuating.

If a check fails

IDSymptomAction
DGas continuously flowing through stone connectionChange the CO2_in solenoid — stuck OPEN.
EGas continuously flowing through arm connectionChange the Head_Transfer solenoid — stuck OPEN.
FGas flowing through both arm and ventChange the Head_Transfer and Vent solenoids — both stuck OPEN.
GFlow meter ball is upSame cause as D — change the CO2_in solenoid, stuck OPEN.
HUp pressed, gas does not pulse through armChange the Head_Transfer solenoid — stuck CLOSED.
IDown pressed, gas does not pulse through stoneFirst confirm the flow meter is not turned all the way off — rotate the knob counterclockwise and retest. If it still does not pulse, change the CO2_in solenoid, stuck CLOSED.
JEnter pressed, no clicking soundConfirm the vent valve is open, then change the Vent solenoid — stuck CLOSED.
