Check 4 repeats the solenoid tests with the CO2 supply moved to the ARM port. Feeding gas in from the other direction exercises paths that Check 3 cannot reach — in particular it is the only test that proves the vent path actually flows.

Step 4: Move the CO2 connection

Disconnect the CO2 push-to-connect from CO2_In and reconnect it to ARM.

Check 4.1: at rest, nothing should flow

With no buttons pressed, confirm no gas is flowing from:

On check L: the CO2 supply is connected to this port, so flow out of it is not physically possible. Treat L as a check that the connection is properly seated and not leaking around the fitting.

Checks 4.2 to 4.4: button tests

Note that the expected results are effectively swapped compared with Check 3 — that reversal is the point of the test.

IDPress and holdExpected result
OUpNo gas flows from anywhere — you should hear a quiet click
PDownGas pulses out of the Stone connection, and the flow meter ball bounces
QEnterGas pulses from the Vent valve

Check Q is the important one. It is the only point in the Health Check where gas should actually come out of the vent, so it is the only test that will catch a vent solenoid stuck closed.
