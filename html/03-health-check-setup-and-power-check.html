This is the first step of the FizzWizz Health Check. Getting the setup right matters — several later checks assume the hoses start disconnected, and testing with them attached will produce false failures.

Step 1: Setup

The FizzWizz power must be OFF before you begin.

  1. Disconnect the stone hose.
  2. Disconnect the arm hose.
  3. Connect CO2 from the regulator to CO2_In.
  4. Set the regulator to approximately 30 to 40 psi.
  5. Ensure the temperature sensor is connected.

Check 1: Wall-wart power supply

With the power supply connected, the green light on the side of the enclosure should be ON.

CheckCriterionResult
1Green power light is ONYes / No

If the green light is off

The FizzWizz will not turn on. Replace the plug-in power supply.

Before replacing it, confirm the outlet itself is live and the barrel connector is fully seated — those account for a fair share of apparent power supply failures.

Next step

Once the green light is confirmed, continue to How to Put the FizzWizz into Test Mode.
