## Configure notification delivery

The **Notifications** page (**Notification Preferences**) controls how alerts reach you. Your channels are personal: each person in your organization sets up their own, and every alert goes to everyone's channels that are switched on.

## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="alert">

The **Notifications** menu item appears when your plan includes at least one of alert rules, email, SMS or push notifications. If it is missing, see [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

</Callout>

## Available channels

| Channel | Plan feature needed | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-app** | In-app alerts | Alerts appear on the Alerts page and in the bell. Shown as **In-App Notifications · Included with your plan · Active**. Nothing to set up. |
| **Email** | Email notifications | Alerts are emailed to each address you add. |
| **SMS** | SMS notifications | Alerts are texted to each number you add and verify. |
| **Push** | Push notifications | Alerts appear as notifications on each phone or computer you turn push on for, even when BrewWizz is closed. |

If nobody in your organization has added any channels, alerts are emailed to your organization's contact address. When you have no channels of your own, the page shows **You haven't set up any channels. If no one in your organization has, alerts go to your organization's contact email.** Once anyone in your organization adds a channel, even one that's switched off, the contact address stops getting alerts.

## Adding an email address

1. Select **+ Add Channel** and choose **Email**.
2. Enter the address and select **Add**.

You can add more than one address, such as your own and a shared brewery inbox.

## Adding and verifying a phone number for SMS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the number">

  Select **+ Add Channel**, choose **SMS**, and enter the mobile number in international format, for example `+15551234567`.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Give consent">

  Tick **I agree to receive automated BrewWizz device alert text messages at the number I provide.** The full SMS terms appear under the checkbox. Then select **Add**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the number">

  Select **Text me a code to verify this number**. Enter the 6-digit code from the text message and select **Verify**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

The number shows **Not verified** and receives no alerts until you verify it. Each code works for 10 minutes and allows 5 tries. Use **Send a new code** if it expires. You can request up to 5 codes an hour.

<Callout kind="info">

No code after a minute? If that number has ever texted STOP to BrewWizz Alerts, text **START** to the same number, then select **Send a new code**.

</Callout>

## SMS keywords: STOP, START and HELP

Reply to any BrewWizz Alerts text with one of these words:

| Keyword (any of) | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **STOP**, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT | Unsubscribes that number from all BrewWizz SMS |
| **START**, UNSTOP, YES | Opts the number back in |
| **HELP**, INFO | Replies with help information. SMS support: [alerts@allendale.biz](mailto:alerts@allendale.biz). |

BrewWizz processes these automatically. After you text STOP, the SMS switch for that number is locked, and the dashboard explains that only texting **START** can turn it back on.

<Callout kind="tip">

SMS opt-out applies to the **phone number**, not to one device. Texting STOP stops alert texts for every device in your organization to that number.

</Callout>

## Push notifications on this device

Push sends alerts to the phone or computer you are using, even when BrewWizz is closed.

1. Open **Notifications** on the phone or computer you want alerts on.
2. In **Push notifications on this device**, select **Turn on for this device**.
3. When your browser or phone asks, allow notifications. A test notification, **Notifications are on for this device.**, confirms it worked.

On iPhone and iPad (iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later), you must first add BrewWizz to your Home Screen and open it from there. The Notifications page shows install steps for the device you are on. See [Install & Sign In](/mobile-app/getting-started) and [Push Notifications](/mobile-app/notifications).

Each phone or computer you turn on is listed as a **Push** channel labelled with its type, such as *iPhone*, *Android* or *Windows*. The one you are using is marked **This device**. You can have up to 10 devices. Signing out of BrewWizz removes push from that device.

## Managing your channels

- **Pause a channel** with its switch.
- **Remove a channel** with the bin icon, then confirm **Remove notification channel?**.
- **Device offline notices:** tick **Also tell me when a device stops reporting for 2 hours** on any channel. BrewWizz checks every 15 minutes and sends one notice per outage. It does not keep sending notices about devices that have been silent for more than 7 days. Offline notices do not appear in the alert history.

## Display units

The Notifications page also sets your display units. Pick **Metric** (°C, bar, g/L) or **US** (°F, psi, volumes), or set each unit separately, then select **Save units**. **Use org default** goes back to your organization's units. Alert messages you receive use your units.

## When your plan changes

If your plan stops including a channel, or your subscription lapses, BrewWizz stops sending alerts on it and its row shows **Not on plan**. You can still switch it off or remove it, but you cannot switch it back on until your plan includes that channel again.
