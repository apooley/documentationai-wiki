## Batches, targets and reports

Each time a FizzWizz starts a new carbonation run, the dashboard records a new **batch**. The batch keeps its own name, carbonation target, notes and history, so you can follow it while it carbonates and look back at it later.

All of this lives on the device page, in the **Batch** card and on the **This batch** chart range. To open a device page, select a tank on the [Devices](/dashboard/devices) page.

## The Batch card

The **Batch** card shows one batch at a time. It starts on the tank's current batch.

- **Choose another batch.** When the tank has more than one batch, pick one from the list at the top of the card. The current batch is marked **(current)**.
- **Name it.** Select the pencil next to **Name** and type a name, such as the beer and brew number (up to 60 characters). Until you name it, a batch is called **Batch N** or **Batch of** its start date.
- **Started** and **Start temperature** show when the run began and how cold the beer was.
- **Carbonation target** shows the batch's target range. See [Setting a carbonation target](#setting-a-carbonation-target).
- **View this batch's charts →** opens the Charts tab on the **This batch** range.
- **Batch report →** opens a printable report. See [Batch reports](#batch-reports).
- **Notes** keep a record of the batch. See [Notes](#notes).

## Setting a carbonation target

The carbonation target is the range you want the batch to finish in. You set it yourself; you don't need to ask BrewWizz support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the target editor">

  On the device page, find **Carbonation target** in the **Batch** card and select the pencil next to it.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a style, or type your own range">

  Choose a style from **Style** to fill in a typical range, or leave it on **Custom** and type **Target min** and **Target max** yourself. The boxes use your carbonation unit (volumes or g/L).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">

  Select **Save target**. To remove a target, open the editor and select **Clear target**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

The style presets, in volumes of CO₂:

| Style | Target range |
| --- | --- |
| British ale | 1.5–2.0 |
| Porter / Stout | 1.7–2.3 |
| Belgian ale | 1.9–2.4 |
| Lager / Pale ale / IPA | 2.2–2.7 |
| American lager | 2.5–2.8 |
| Fruit lambic | 3.0–4.5 |
| Wheat beer | 3.3–4.5 |

A target must be above 0 and no more than 6 volumes, and you need both a minimum and a maximum.

### How targets carry over

- **The current batch's target is the tank's target.** The overview, the gauges, the **Needs attention** filter and the alerts all use it. The editor reminds you: **This is the tank's current target; its next batch starts with it.**
- **A new batch starts with the previous batch's target**, so a tank that always runs the same beer only needs setting once.
- **Past batches keep the target they had.** Changing a past batch's target changes that batch only, for example to correct its report.
- **A tank with no batches yet** shows the target editor on its own, under the Batch card. The tank's first batch starts with that target.

## Time to target and ready

While a batch is below its target, the device page, the tank's card on the Devices page and its Board view tile show an estimate of when it will get there:

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Reaches target in ~5 h (around 3:40 PM)** | Carbonation is climbing steadily. The estimate comes from the last hour of readings in this batch. |
| **ETA after a few more readings** | The batch has just started, or there are not enough recent readings yet. |
| **Not rising: no ETA** | Carbonation is flat or falling. |
| **Too unsteady for an ETA** | The readings jump around too much for a reliable estimate. |
| **Rising slowly: more than 3 days to target at this rate** | The batch is climbing, but very slowly. |

No estimate shows when the tank has no target, or when carbonation is already above it.

A batch counts as **ready** once its carbonation has stayed inside its target for 2 hours. The device page then shows **Ready since** with the day and time, and the tank's tile on the [Board view](/dashboard/devices#board-view-and-cellar-screen) says **Ready** and **Ready since**. If you change the batch's target, the ready time is cleared so the batch can become ready again against the new target.

<Callout kind="tip">

You can be told when a batch is ready. Turn on **Also tell me when a batch is ready** for an email, SMS or push channel in [Notification Preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences#batch-ready-notices). It is off until you turn it on.

</Callout>

## The This batch chart

Select **This batch** above the charts, or **View this batch's charts →** in the Batch card, to chart the selected batch from its start to its end (or to now, for the current batch).

- The **Carbonation** chart shades this batch's own target, labelled with the batch name, for example **Kölsch 12 target 2.40–2.70 vol**. On the other time ranges, the chart shades the tank's current target instead.
- The **Batches** tab shows **Carbonation at batch start** and **Latest carbonation** (or **End carbonation** for a past batch) when the This batch range is selected.

### Comparing with a past batch

On the **This batch** range, when the tank has other batches, **Compare with** appears above the charts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a batch to compare with">

  Choose one of the tank's other batches from **Compare with**. The list shows each batch's name and start date, newest first.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the overlay">

  The **Carbonation**, **Temperature** and **Tank pressure** charts each show both batches. The comparison batch is drawn as a second line, and both are lined up by time since each batch started, so you can see which one carbonated faster.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the time to target">

  **Time to reach target** above the charts shows how long each batch took to reach its own target, and how much faster or slower this batch was. A batch that never reached its target, or had none, says so.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Choose **None** to go back to the normal charts. Zooming is not available while comparing.

## Batch reports

A batch report is a one-page record of a batch for your quality files. Open it with **Batch report →** in the Batch card.

The report shows your brewery name, the batch name, the tank and its serial number, and the date it was printed. It contains:

- **Summary**: **Started**, **Ended** (or **In progress**), **Duration**, **Readings**, **Carbonation target**, **Time to reach target**, **Ready (held in target for 2 h)**, the **Final carbonation** (or **Latest carbonation** for a batch still running), the temperature minimum, average and maximum, and the tank pressure and CO₂ supply ranges
- **Carbonation**: a chart of the whole batch with its target shaded
- **Alerts during this batch**
- **Notes**

Select **Print / Save as PDF** to print it, or choose **Save as PDF** in your browser's print window to keep a copy. Values use your [display units](/dashboard/overview#display-units). Select **Back to** the tank name to return to the device page.

## Notes

Use **Notes** in the Batch card to record what happened during a batch, such as dry hopping, a CO₂ cylinder change or a tasting result. Type in **Add a note** and select **Add note**.

- Notes can be up to 2,000 characters, and the newest is listed first.
- Everyone in your organization can see them, and they appear on the batch report.
- Notes cannot be edited. You can delete notes you wrote yourself.
