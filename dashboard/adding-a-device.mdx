## Adding and claiming a device

A FizzWizz has to belong to your organization before it appears in the dashboard and before its readings are checked against your alert rules. You link it by **claiming** it with a 6-digit code that the unit shows on its display.

## What you need

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **The unit online** | Connected to your network and reporting. See [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account). |
| **Its claim code** | 6 digits, shown on the FizzWizz display after it first connects |
| **An active subscription** | With room in your device allowance. The Billing page shows **Devices: N of LIMIT**. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing). |

## Claiming the device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the unit">

  Power the FizzWizz and connect it to your network. See [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Write down the claim code">

  When the unit first reports to BrewWizz, its display shows **CLAIM CODE:** followed by 6 digits. The display shows the code for about a minute, so write it down. The code stays valid for **30 days**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code in the dashboard">

  Sign in and go to **Devices**. In the **Claim a device** card, type the 6 digits into **Claim code** and select **Claim device**. A **Claimed** message with the serial number confirms it worked.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the device">

  Open the new device and select the pencil next to its name. Use the tank or vessel it serves.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Name devices by the tank or vessel they serve, not by serial number. When an alert fires at 2 a.m., `Brite Tank 2` is far more useful than `FW-0413`. Names can be up to 60 characters. If you clear the name, the dashboard shows the serial number instead.

</Callout>

BrewWizz can also assign a device to your organization for you. If you would rather do that, contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) with the serial number from the unit's label.

## If the claim fails

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **You missed the code on the display** | Restarting the unit does not show the code again. Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) with the serial number, and BrewWizz will issue a new code or assign the device to you. |
| **That code isn't right. Check the 6 digits on the device's screen.** | Check for a typo and try again. |
| **That code has expired. The device shows a new one after its next report.** | Codes last 30 days. Wait for the unit to report again, then use the new code on its display. |
| **That device already belongs to an organization.** | Someone has already claimed it. If it should be yours, contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) with the serial number. |
| **Your organization needs an active BrewWizz subscription to manage devices.** | Start or renew your subscription on [Billing](/dashboard/billing). |
| **Your subscription covers N device(s). Add a device to your subscription to claim another.** | Add a device to your subscription in the billing portal, or release a device you no longer use. |
| **Too many claim attempts** | Each person can try 20 codes every 15 minutes, and your organization 50 a day. Wait and try again later, or contact support. |
| **The device is claimed but shows Stale or Offline** | It is claimed but not reporting. See [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline). |

## After claiming

1. **[Check its health](/dashboard/devices)** to confirm it is Online.
2. **[Open its detail page](/dashboard/device-detail)** for gauges, charts and batches.
3. **[Set a carbonation target](/dashboard/batches-and-targets#setting-a-carbonation-target)** so the dashboard can show when each batch will be ready.
4. **[Set alert rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules)** for its readings.
5. **[Choose how alerts reach you](/dashboard/notification-preferences)**.

## Releasing a device

Each device card on the Devices page has a **Release device** button. BrewWizz asks you to confirm (**Release** followed by the device's name); select **Release** to go ahead.

<Callout kind="alert">

Once confirmed, the device leaves your organization right away and **its alert rules are deleted**. It disappears from your list. The next time it reports, its display shows a new claim code, so anyone who can see its display could claim it. To bring it back, claim it again with the new code and set up its alert rules again.

</Callout>

## Moving or replacing a device

- **Moving a unit to a different tank:** rename it so the dashboard still shows what it serves.
- **Replacing a unit:** claim the new unit, then release the old one so it no longer counts towards your device allowance.
