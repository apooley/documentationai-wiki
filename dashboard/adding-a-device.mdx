## Adding and claiming a device

A FizzWizz has to be assigned to your organisation before it appears anywhere in BrewWizz. This page covers that link — the connection between the hardware you installed and the account you monitor it from.

## What you need

| Requirement | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **Serial number** | On the FizzWizz label |
| **Device name** | What you want to call it in the dashboard — use something recognisable, such as the tank it serves |
| Organisation assignment | Your account must already be assigned to an organisation — see [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in) |
| Network connection | The unit must be online — see [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account) |

<Callout kind="tip">

Name devices by the tank or vessel they serve, not by serial number. When an alert fires at 2 a.m., `Brite Tank 2` is far more useful than `FW-0413`.

</Callout>

## Claiming the device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the unit is online">

  The FizzWizz must be connected to your network and reporting before it can be claimed. See [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to the dashboard">

  Sign in at [brewwizzdata.onesmartview.com/auth/login](https://brewwizzdata.onesmartview.com/auth/login).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the device">

  Add the FizzWizz using its serial number, and give it a recognisable name.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it appears">

  The device should now appear in your device list with a health badge. **Online** means it has reported within the last 10 minutes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

If the device does not appear, work through the checks below.

## If the device does not appear

| Check | What to confirm | Where to go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Is the unit online?** | Green power light on, unit reporting | [Power Connection](/setup/power) · [Network & Account Setup](/setup/network-and-account) |
| **Is the serial number correct?** | Match against the label on the unit | — |
| **Is your account assigned to an organisation?** | Without this, nothing appears at all | [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in) |
| **Was the device claimed for a different email?** | The claim must be made from the account that will use it | [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in) |
| **Has the list refreshed?** | The dashboard updates automatically, but refresh to be sure | — |
| **Is the device shown as Stale or Offline?** | It is claimed but not reporting | [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline) |

<Callout kind="alert">

If **no** devices appear — not just this one — the most likely cause is that your account is not assigned to an organisation. See [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in).

</Callout>

## After claiming

Once the device is listed and reporting:

1. **[Review its health state](/dashboard/devices)** to confirm it is Online.
2. **[Open the device detail page](/dashboard/device-detail)** to see its telemetry charts and metadata.
3. **[Configure alert rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules)** for that device's thresholds.
4. **[Set notification preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences)** so alerts reach you.
5. **[Check your plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan)** if alert features are missing.

## Moving or replacing a device

If you relocate a FizzWizz to a different tank, update its name so the dashboard still reflects what it serves. If you replace a unit, claim the new serial number and remove or rename the old entry so you are not alerted on a device that no longer exists.
