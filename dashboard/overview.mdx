## BrewWizz Dashboard at a glance

The BrewWizz dashboard is where you monitor your FizzWizz units. It shows live gauges and charts for every tank, raises alerts when a reading goes out of range, and holds your subscription details. It runs in any modern browser at [bw-dash.allendale.biz](https://bw-dash.allendale.biz). You can also install it on your phone or computer as an app; see [Mobile App Overview](/mobile-app/overview).

<Callout kind="info">

The BrewWizz Dashboard supports **FizzWizz devices only**. PacWizz devices do not report to the dashboard. If you use PacWizz, see the [PacWizz User Guide](/pacwizz/overview).

</Callout>

## Navigation

| Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Devices** | Your device list, with live gauges for every FizzWizz in your organization. This is also where you claim a new device. Open a device for charts, readings, batches, notes and alert rules. |
| **Alerts** | Alert history. Acknowledge alerts here. |
| **Notifications** | Choose how alerts reach you: email, SMS, or push notifications on this phone or computer. You can also set your display units here. |
| **API** | Create read-only [API keys](/dashboard/api-keys) for scripts and integrations. |
| **Billing** | Your plan, device allowance and subscription status. |
| **Help** | Opens this help center in a new tab. |

The header also holds the alert bell, the unit selectors, your name and a sign-out button. On a phone, everything except the bell moves into the menu (☰) at the top right.

<Callout kind="info">

**Alerts**, **Notifications** and **API** depend on your plan. When your plan does not include them, **Upgrade** appears in their place. See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

</Callout>

## Device health states

Health is based only on how long it has been since BrewWizz last received a reading from the device.

| State | Last reading | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Less than 10 minutes ago | Green | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | 10–120 minutes ago | Yellow | Reporting intermittently |
| **Offline** | More than 120 minutes ago, or never | Red | Not reporting |

See [Devices & Health States](/dashboard/devices) for what each state means in practice.

## Display units

You can show temperature in **°C or °F**, pressure in **bar or psi**, and carbonation in **g/L or volumes** (1 volume = 1.96 g/L). Change them with the unit selectors in the header. They are also on the Notifications page and on each device page, under **Display units**.

Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to every browser and to the installed app. It applies to gauges, charts, alert thresholds, your alert messages and CSV exports. If you have not chosen units, the dashboard uses your organization's default.

## How readings flow

1. Your FizzWizz sends a reading to BrewWizz over your network.
2. BrewWizz checks it and stores it.
3. BrewWizz compares it against that device's alert rules, if your plan includes them, and creates an alert when a threshold is crossed.
4. Alerts go out by email, SMS or push, depending on your plan and your [notification preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences).

The dashboard updates live as new readings and alerts arrive, so you do not need to refresh the page.

## Welcome banner and offline bar

- The first time you sign in, a **Welcome to the new BrewWizz dashboard** banner lists some quick tips. Once you dismiss it, it stays hidden in that browser.
- If your connection drops, an amber bar reads **You are offline — data may be outdated**. Buttons that save changes are greyed out until you reconnect.
