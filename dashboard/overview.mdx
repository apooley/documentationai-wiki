## BrewWizz Dashboard at a glance

The BrewWizz dashboard is where you monitor your FizzWizz units. It shows live gauges and charts for every tank, tells you when a batch will reach its carbonation target, raises alerts when something goes wrong, and holds your subscription details. It runs in any modern browser at [dash.brewwizz.com](https://dash.brewwizz.com). You can also install it on your phone or computer as an app; see [Mobile App Overview](/mobile-app/overview).

<Callout kind="info">

The BrewWizz Dashboard supports **FizzWizz devices only**. PacWizz devices do not report to the dashboard. If you use PacWizz, see the [PacWizz User Guide](/pacwizz/overview).

</Callout>

## Navigation

| Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Devices** | Your device list, with live gauges for every FizzWizz in your organization, filters and search. This is also where you claim a new device, and where **Board view** opens a big-screen view of every tank. Open a device for charts, readings, batches, carbonation targets, notes and alert rules. |
| **Alerts** | Alert history, and the on/off switches for automatic alerts. Acknowledge alerts here. |
| **Notifications** | Choose how alerts reach you: email, SMS, or push notifications on this phone or computer. You can also turn on batch ready notices and the weekly summary email, and set your display units here. |
| **API** | Create read-only [API keys](/dashboard/api-keys) for scripts and integrations. |
| **Billing** | Your plan, device allowance and subscription status. |
| **Help** | Opens this help center in a new tab. |

The header also holds the alert bell, the unit selectors, your name and a sign-out button.

On a phone, a tab bar along the bottom of the screen holds **Devices**, **Alerts** and **Notifications** (or **Upgrade**), plus **More** for API, Billing, Help, display units and **Sign out**. The alert bell stays at the top.

<Callout kind="info">

**Alerts**, **Notifications** and **API** depend on your plan. When your plan does not include alerts, **Upgrade** appears instead. See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

</Callout>

## Device health states

Health is based only on how long it has been since BrewWizz last received a reading from the device.

| State | Last reading | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Less than 10 minutes ago | Green | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | 10–120 minutes ago | Yellow | Reporting intermittently |
| **Offline** | More than 120 minutes ago, or never | Red | Not reporting |

See [Devices & Health States](/dashboard/devices) for what each state means in practice.

## Display units

You can show temperature in **°C or °F**, pressure in **bar or psi**, and carbonation in **g/L or volumes** (1 volume = 1.96 g/L). Change them with the unit selectors in the header (in **More** on a phone). They are also on the Notifications page and on each device page, under **Display units**.

Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to every browser and to the installed app. It applies to gauges, charts, carbonation targets, alert thresholds, your alert messages, batch reports, the weekly summary and CSV exports. If you have not chosen units, the dashboard uses your organization's default.

## How readings flow

1. Your FizzWizz sends a reading to BrewWizz over your network.
2. BrewWizz checks it and stores it.
3. BrewWizz compares it against that device's alert rules, if your plan includes them, and watches the tank's recent trend for [automatic alerts](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules#automatic-alerts). It creates an alert when something is wrong.
4. Alerts go out by email, SMS or push, depending on your plan and your [notification preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences).

## Keeping the screen up to date

- The dashboard updates live as new readings and alerts arrive, so you do not need to refresh the page.
- The Devices page and each device page show **Updated … ago**, so you can see how fresh the values are. Hover over it for the exact time.
- If you switch away from the dashboard for more than about 30 seconds, for example by locking your phone, it reloads as soon as you come back or your connection returns.
- Times such as **Last reading 3 min ago** count up on their own. Hover over one (or press and hold on a phone) to see the exact date and time.
- The browser tab shows which page you are on, such as **Alerts** or a tank's name, so several open tabs are easy to tell apart.

## Welcome banner and offline bar

- The first time you sign in, a **Welcome to the new BrewWizz dashboard** banner lists some quick tips. Once you dismiss it, it stays hidden in that browser.
- If your connection drops, an amber bar reads **You are offline — data may be outdated**. Buttons that save changes are greyed out until you reconnect.
