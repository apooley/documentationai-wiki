## Signing in and account setup

The BrewWizz dashboard runs at [dash.brewwizz.com](https://dash.brewwizz.com). There is no sign-up page. BrewWizz creates accounts and emails each person an invitation. To get an account for yourself or a teammate, email [info@brewwizz.com](mailto:info@brewwizz.com).

## Accepting your invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">

  Select the link in the email from BrewWizz.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">

  The **Set your password** page opens. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters, enter it again under **Confirm password**, and select **Save password**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="You are signed in">

  The dashboard opens on your **Devices** page.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing in

1. Go to [dash.brewwizz.com](https://dash.brewwizz.com).
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on that browser until you sign out. If your session ends, the dashboard shows **Your session expired. Please sign in again.** and takes you back to the sign-in page.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Incorrect email or password. Please try again. | Check both and try again, or reset your password |
| Too many sign-in attempts. Please wait a moment and try again. | Wait a few minutes before trying again |
| Please confirm your email before signing in | Open the invitation email and follow its link first |

## Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and select the link. The dashboard shows the message below whether or not that address has an account, so it never reveals which addresses are registered:

   > If an account exists for that email, you will receive a reset link shortly.

4. On the **Set your password** page, choose a new password of at least 8 characters and select **Save password**.

<Callout kind="info">

If you use the installed [mobile app](/mobile-app/getting-started), the reset link opens in your browser. Finish the reset there, then sign in to the app with your new password.

</Callout>

## Accounts and organizations

Every account belongs to one **organization**, usually your brewery. Everyone in the organization sees the same devices, alerts, batch notes and billing, and everyone can do the same things: claim and rename devices, name batches and set carbonation targets, set alert rules, turn automatic alerts on or off, manage API keys and view billing. There are no separate owner or viewer roles.

Some settings are personal to you:

- your notification channels (email addresses, phone numbers, push devices)
- your display units
- the [weekly summary email](/dashboard/notification-preferences#weekly-summary-email), which goes to the email address you sign in with. If that address changes, the summary stops until you turn it on again.

To add or remove people in your organization, contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).

## If the dashboard shows only Billing

The dashboard needs an active BrewWizz subscription. If your organization does not have one, every page shows only **Billing**, along with this message:

> Your organization doesn't have an active BrewWizz subscription, so the dashboard is unavailable.

This happens when there has never been a subscription, when it was cancelled, or when a payment has been overdue for more than 30 days. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing) to subscribe or renew. Your devices keep recording readings in the meantime.

## Account setup incomplete

If you see **Account setup incomplete — Your account profile is missing**, select **Retry**. If the message comes back, sign out and contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).

## After signing in

1. **[Claim your devices](/dashboard/adding-a-device)** so they appear in your device list.
2. **[Check their health](/dashboard/devices)** to confirm they are reporting.
3. **[Set alert rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules)** for the readings that matter to you.
4. **[Choose how alerts reach you](/dashboard/notification-preferences)**.
5. **[Install the app](/mobile-app/getting-started)** on your phone for push notifications.
