## Signing in and account setup

The BrewWizz dashboard runs at [brewwizzdata.onesmartview.com/auth/login](https://brewwizzdata.onesmartview.com/auth/login). Before you can see any devices, two things need to be true: you have an account, and that account is assigned to an organisation.

## Signing in

1. Go to [brewwizzdata.onesmartview.com/auth/login](https://brewwizzdata.onesmartview.com/auth/login).
2. Sign in with your email address and password.
3. You land on your organisation's dashboard.

<Callout kind="info">

If you have forgotten your password, use the reset flow. After completing the reset in your browser, sign in again with the new password.

</Callout>

## Accounts and organisations

BrewWizz organises everything around **organisations**. Devices belong to an organisation; users are assigned to an organisation. You see the devices, alerts, and billing for the organisation your account is assigned to.

<Callout kind="alert">

**The no-organization state cannot be bypassed.** If your account has not been assigned to an organisation, the dashboard and mobile app will not show any devices, alerts, or telemetry — no matter how many FizzWizz units you have installed and online.

This is the most common cause of "no devices showing", and it is easy to mistake for a device problem. If your devices are missing entirely, check this first.

</Callout>

### Who assigns accounts

Organisation assignment is done by an administrator for your organisation. If you are not sure who that is, contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).

### After you are assigned

Once your administrator confirms the assignment, refresh the dashboard. The dashboard updates automatically when your assignment or plan changes — you do not need to sign out and back in.

## What you see depends on your plan

The dashboard adapts to your plan and subscription status. Menu items you expect may be missing:

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** menu item missing | Your plan does not include alert rules, email notifications, or SMS notifications |
| **Upgrade** shown in the menu | You do not have an active or trial subscription |
| Device alert rules are read-only | Your plan includes in-app alerts only |

See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan) for the full feature matrix, and [Billing & Subscriptions](/dashboard/billing) to start or fix a subscription.

## After signing in

Once you can see the dashboard, the usual next steps are:

1. **[Add and claim your devices](/dashboard/adding-a-device)** so they appear in your device list.
2. **[Review device health](/dashboard/devices)** to confirm they are reporting.
3. **[Configure alert rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules)** for the thresholds that matter to you.
4. **[Set notification preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences)** so alerts reach you.

## Signing in to the mobile app

The [mobile app](/mobile-app/getting-started) uses the same account and shows the same devices and alerts. It is currently in development.

If the app signs you in but you stay on a **No organization** screen, the cause is the same organisation assignment described above.
