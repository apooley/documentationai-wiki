## Devices and health states

The device list is the dashboard's home view. It shows every FizzWizz in your organisation and its current health at a glance.

## The device list

Each device appears with:

- Its **name** — ideally the tank or vessel it serves
- Its **health badge** — Online, Stale, or Offline
- Its **latest readings** — current pressure and temperature values

Selecting a device opens its [detail page](/dashboard/device-detail) with charts, readings, metadata, and alert rules.

## Device health states

Health is determined entirely by **how long it has been since the last reported reading**. It is a measure of reporting, not of whether the unit is carbonating correctly.

| State | Last reading age | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Less than 10 minutes | Green | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | 10–120 minutes | Yellow | Intermittent reporting |
| **Offline** | More than 120 minutes | Red | Not reporting |

<Callout kind="info">

A device showing **Stale** may recover on its own. Wait 10 minutes before investigating.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

A green **Online** badge does **not** mean carbonation is on target. It only means the unit is reporting. A FizzWizz can be reporting perfectly while carbonation drifts — that is what [alert rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules) are for. See [Understanding Your Readings](/dashboard/readings-explained) for what to actually watch.

</Callout>

## Why a device goes Stale or Offline

| Cause | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Power interruption** | The unit lost power, or the power supply failed |
| **Network connectivity** | Wi-Fi dropped, or signal is marginal at the mounting location |
| **Firmware update** | The device is briefly unavailable during an update |
| **Relocation** | The unit was moved and has not reconnected |
| **Account or organisation change** | Device assignment changed |

Full diagnosis is on [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

## Finding a device

As your fleet grows, use search and filtering to find a specific unit:

- **Search** by device name or serial number
- **Filter** by health state to see only the devices needing attention

In the [mobile app](/mobile-app/devices), the same filtering is available with the same health states, so a device showing **Stale** on your phone means the same thing as on the desktop.

## What to do with a red device

1. Confirm whether carbonation is actually affected, or whether it is only reporting that stopped. The unit carbonates without the network.
2. Work through [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).
3. Once it reconnects, review its [charts](/dashboard/device-detail) for the gap period to confirm the batch stayed on track.
