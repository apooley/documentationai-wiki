## Devices and health states

The **Devices** page (titled **My Devices**) is the dashboard's home view. It shows every FizzWizz in your organization as a card with live gauges. The **Claim a device** card for adding a new unit is also on this page; see [Adding & Claiming a Device](/dashboard/adding-a-device).

Next to the title, **Board view** opens a big-screen view of every tank (see [Board view and cellar screen](#board-view-and-cellar-screen)), and **Updated … ago** shows how fresh the page is.

## What each card shows

| Element | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The name you gave the device, or its serial number if it has no name. The serial number and firmware version appear underneath. |
| **Health pill** | Online, Stale or Offline |
| **Gauges** | Temperature, Tank pressure (with a tick at the target pressure) and CO₂ supply |
| **Carbonation** | The carbonation level the unit reports, and **Δ pressure**, the gap between tank and target pressure |
| **Pressure control chip** | **On setpoint** (tank pressure within 0.05 bar / 0.7 psi of target), **Near setpoint** (within 0.15 bar / 2.2 psi), or **Off setpoint** |
| **Carbonation target chip** | **Below target**, **In target band** or **Above target**, when the tank has a [carbonation target](/dashboard/batches-and-targets#setting-a-carbonation-target) |
| **Time to target** | While the batch is below its target, an estimate such as **Reaches target in ~5 h (around 3:40 PM)** |
| **Last reading** | How long ago the device last reported, such as **Last reading 3 min ago**, or **Never**. Hover over it (or press and hold on a phone) for the exact date and time. |
| **Release device** | Removes the device from your organization. See [Releasing a device](/dashboard/adding-a-device#releasing-a-device). |

Select a card to open the device's [detail page](/dashboard/device-detail). To rename a device, open it and select the pencil next to its name.

## Filtering and searching

Above the cards, filter buttons narrow the list. Each shows how many tanks it matches:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every device |
| **Needs attention** | Devices with a red or amber border (see below) |
| **Carbonating** | Devices below their carbonation target that reported in the last 30 minutes |
| **Ready** | Devices whose carbonation is inside their target |
| **Offline** | Devices with no reading for 2 hours |

A filter only appears when it matches at least one tank. When you have more than 6 devices, a **Search by name or serial** box appears too. It matches any part of a tank's name or serial number.

If nothing matches, the page reads **No tanks match** and offers **Show all** to clear the filter and search.

The chosen filter and search are kept in the page address, so reloading the page, or going back to it from a device, keeps your view. You can bookmark a filtered view, such as **Needs attention**, or send the link to a colleague in your organization.

## Devices that need attention

The page lists devices that need attention first, and shows a count such as **2 devices need attention · shown first**. The card border shows why:

| Border | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Red** | The device is Offline |
| **Amber** | The device is Stale, tank pressure is **Off setpoint**, or carbonation is outside its target |
| **None** | Reporting normally and on target |

Within each group, devices are sorted by name. The page updates live, so a card moves as soon as its state changes.

## Board view and cellar screen

**Board view** (the **Tank board** page) shows every tank as a large tile you can read from across the cellar. Open it with **Board view** next to the **My Devices** title, and go back with **All devices**.

Each tile shows:

- the tank name
- one status word: **Offline**, **Stale**, **Below target**, **Above target**, **Off setpoint**, **Ready**, **In target band**, **On setpoint**, **Near setpoint** or **Online**, in that order of importance
- the carbonation in large type, with **Target** and its range, or **No target set**
- the temperature
- the time-to-target estimate, or **Ready since** once the batch has held its target for 2 hours
- **Last reading … ago**

Tanks that need attention come first. Select a tile to open that tank. **Dark theme** switches to light text on a dark background, which is easier to read in a dim cellar.

### Cellar screen

Select **Cellar screen** to turn the board into a wall display, for example on a tablet mounted in the cellar.

- The board fills the whole screen, with no menus. Where the browser allows it, it also goes full screen; select **Full screen** if it didn't.
- The screen is kept awake. If the device doesn't allow that, the page shows **Screen may sleep on this device**; change the device's own sleep setting instead.
- Tiles can't be tapped, so nobody opens a tank by accident.
- The board refreshes itself at least once a minute, as well as updating live.
- Select **Exit cellar screen**, or press **Esc**, to go back.

<Callout kind="tip">

The cellar screen and dark theme are kept in the page address. Bookmark the page while the cellar screen is showing, or add it to the tablet's Home Screen, and it opens straight into the cellar screen next time. The tablet still needs to be signed in to BrewWizz.

</Callout>

## Device health states

Health is determined entirely by **how long it has been since the last reading**. It shows whether the unit is reporting, not whether it is carbonating correctly. The legend at the top of the page is a reminder: *online (< 10 min), stale (10–120 min), offline (> 120 min)*.

| State | Last reading | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Less than 10 minutes ago | Green | Actively reporting |
| **Stale** | 10–120 minutes ago | Yellow | Reporting intermittently |
| **Offline** | More than 120 minutes ago, or never | Red | Not reporting |

<Callout kind="info">

A device showing **Stale** often recovers on its own. Wait 10 minutes before investigating.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

A green **Online** pill does **not** mean carbonation is on target. It only means the unit is reporting. Watch the pressure control and carbonation chips, and set [alert rules](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules) for the readings that matter. See [Understanding Your Readings](/dashboard/readings-explained).

</Callout>

## Why a device goes Stale or Offline

| Cause | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Power interruption** | The unit lost power, or the power supply failed |
| **Network connectivity** | Wi-Fi dropped, or the signal is weak where the unit is mounted |
| **Firmware update** | The device is briefly unavailable during an update |
| **Relocation** | The unit was moved and has not reconnected |

To get an email, text or push notification when a device stops reporting for 2 hours, turn on **Also tell me when a device stops reporting for 2 hours** for a channel in [Notification Preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences). Full diagnosis is on [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

## What to do with a red device

1. Check whether carbonation is actually affected, or only the reporting has stopped. The unit keeps carbonating without the network.
2. Work through [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).
3. Once it reconnects, check its [charts](/dashboard/device-detail) for the gap to confirm the batch stayed on track.
