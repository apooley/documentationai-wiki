## How billing works

BrewWizz subscriptions are bought and managed in the **BrewWizz billing portal**, not inside the dashboard. The dashboard's **Billing** page shows your current subscription and links to the portal.

## The Billing page

When your organization has a subscription, the Billing page shows:

| Item | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan name and price** | Your current plan |
| **Status** | active, trialing or past due. See [Subscription statuses](#subscription-statuses). |
| **Renews on** | The date the current billing period ends |
| **Devices: N of LIMIT** | How many devices you have claimed, out of the number your subscription covers |
| **Manage subscription** | Opens the billing portal, where you can update your payment method, change your plan or device count, or cancel |

If BrewWizz set up your subscription directly, for example a complimentary or trial subscription, the page shows **Managed by BrewWizz — contact support for billing changes.** Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) to change it.

## Starting a subscription

If your organization has no subscription, the Billing page shows **Get a subscription**, or **Resubscribe** if you had one before.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe in the billing portal">

  Select **Subscribe at** followed by the portal's address, and complete checkout there. Use your organization's contact email address as the billing email.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Link it to the dashboard">

  Back on the Billing page, select **Check for my subscription**. BrewWizz looks for an active subscription under your organization's contact email and links it. **Subscription linked.** confirms it worked, and the full dashboard unlocks straight away.

  </Step>
</Steps>

| Message after checking | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription linked.** | Done |
| No active subscription was found for your billing email yet. | Checkout may still be processing. Wait a minute and check again. If it still fails, make sure you used your organization's contact email at checkout. |
| Several subscriptions match | Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) to link the right one |
| No billing email on file | Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) to add a contact email to your organization |

## Subscription statuses

| Status | Dashboard access | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active** | Full, per your plan | Your subscription is current. |
| **Trialing** | Full, per your plan | Your trial is running. |
| **Past due**, first 30 days | Full, per your plan, with a warning banner | A payment failed. Renew before the grace period ends. |
| **Past due**, after 30 days | Billing page only | The grace period has ended. |
| **Cancelled** | Billing page only | Your subscription has ended. |
| **Incomplete** | Billing page only | Checkout was not finished. |
| **No subscription** | Billing page only | Subscribe to unlock the dashboard. |

## Overdue payments and the grace period

If a payment fails, your subscription becomes **past due** and you have a **30-day grace period**. Everything keeps working, but every page shows a banner:

> Your subscription payment is overdue. Renew by *date* to keep using your dashboard.

Select **Renew now** in the banner, or **Renew or update payment method** on the Billing page, to fix it in the billing portal.

<Callout kind="alert">

When the grace period ends, the banner says your dashboard is locked. The dashboard then shows only the Billing page until you renew. No alerts are created or sent in the meantime.

</Callout>

## When the dashboard is locked

Without an active subscription, or once the grace period has ended, every page shows only **Billing**, with this message:

> Your organization doesn't have an active BrewWizz subscription, so the dashboard is unavailable.

Subscribe or renew in the billing portal, or email [info@brewwizz.com](mailto:info@brewwizz.com) to sign up. The dashboard unlocks as soon as the subscription is active.

## Device allowance

Your subscription covers a set number of devices, shown as **Devices: N of LIMIT**. When you reach the limit, claiming another device fails with *Your subscription covers N device(s). Add a device to your subscription to claim another.* Add devices to your subscription in the billing portal, or release a device you no longer use.

## What happens to your data

Your devices keep sending readings, and BrewWizz keeps storing them, even while your subscription is lapsed.

| Situation | How long readings and batches are kept |
| --- | --- |
| Devices claimed by an organization with an active subscription | 5 years |
| Organization without an active subscription for 90 days or more | The most recent 180 days |
| Readings recorded while a device was unclaimed | 90 days |
