## What your plan includes

Every BrewWizz subscription includes the dashboard itself: your devices, live gauges, charts, readings, the Board view and cellar screen, batches with carbonation targets and time-to-target estimates, comparing batches, batch reports, notes, CSV export and the optional weekly summary email. Your plan then decides which of these six features you get:

| Feature | What it gives you | Where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-app alerts** | Alert history, the alert bell and the switches for [automatic alerts](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules#automatic-alerts) | **Alerts** menu item, bell in the header |
| **Alert rules** | Create alert thresholds for each device | The **Alerts** tab on each device page |
| **Email notifications** | Alerts sent to email addresses you add | **Notifications** |
| **SMS notifications** | Alerts texted to phone numbers you verify | **Notifications** |
| **Push notifications** | Alerts on your phone or computer through the installed app | **Notifications** |
| **API access** | Read-only [API keys](/dashboard/api-keys) for scripts and integrations | **API** menu item |

To find out exactly what your plan includes, or to change it, contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) or open the billing portal from [Billing](/dashboard/billing).

## Your subscription status and what you can use

| Subscription status | What you can use |
| --- | --- |
| **Active or trialing** | Everything your plan includes. |
| **Past due, within 30 days** | Everything your plan includes, with a banner asking you to renew. |
| **Past due for more than 30 days, cancelled, incomplete, or no subscription** | The Billing page only. No alerts are created or sent, and no batch ready notices or weekly summary emails go out. Your devices keep recording readings. |

See [Billing](/dashboard/billing) for the grace period and how to renew.

## Why a menu item is missing

<Callout kind="info">

- **Alerts** needs in-app alerts.
- **Notifications** needs at least one of alert rules, email, SMS or push notifications.
- **API** needs API access.

When your plan does not include in-app alerts, **Upgrade** appears in the menu instead of **Alerts** and **Notifications**, and links to Billing. When it does not include API access, **API** is simply not shown. If the dashboard cannot load your plan, it shows **Could not load plan features. Alert navigation may be unavailable.** Select **Retry**.

</Callout>

The dashboard updates automatically when your plan or subscription changes. You do not need to sign out or reload.

## When your plan changes

<Callout kind="alert">

If your plan is downgraded or your subscription lapses:

- **Alert rules** are switched off and kept. When your plan includes alert rules again, turn each rule back on from the device's Alerts tab.
- **Email, SMS or push channels** your plan no longer includes stop receiving alerts and show **Not on plan**. You cannot switch them back on until your plan includes them again.

</Callout>
