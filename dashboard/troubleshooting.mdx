## Common issues and how to fix them

Each section lists the most likely causes first. For problems specific to the installed app on a phone, see [Mobile App Troubleshooting](/mobile-app/troubleshooting).

## Can't sign in

1. Check your email address and password. **Incorrect email or password. Please try again.** means one of them is wrong.
2. If you forgot your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. See [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in#resetting-your-password).
3. **Too many sign-in attempts** means you need to wait a few minutes before trying again.
4. New user? Open the invitation email and set your password first. There is no sign-up page. A manager at your brewery can invite you from the [Team](/dashboard/team) page, or you can email [info@brewwizz.com](mailto:info@brewwizz.com).
5. Removed from the team? A manager can remove people, and a removed person can no longer sign in. Ask a manager at your brewery if that was a mistake, and they can invite you again.

## An invitation didn't arrive, or I can't invite someone

**The person didn't get the email**

1. Ask them to check their spam or junk folder and search for "BrewWizz".
2. Check the address on the **Team** page for typos. If it is wrong, remove the person and invite the right address.
3. Select **Resend invite** next to their name (they must still be marked **Pending**).
4. Invitation links stop working after a while, so ask them to use the newest email. Resending sends a fresh link.
5. Still nothing after a few minutes? Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts).

**The Team page is missing**

Only managers see **Team**. Ask a manager to make you one if you need it. See [Team](/dashboard/team#managers-and-members).

**Invite says it can't invite that address**

For privacy, BrewWizz gives the same message in several cases, so it doesn't reveal who has an account. It appears when the address already has a BrewWizz account (in any brewery), or can't be used. Try a different address, or contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) if you believe the person needs to move to your brewery.

**Invite isn't available**

| Message or situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your brewery has reached its limit of 10 people | Remove someone who has left. Pending invitations count. |
| Too many invitations sent today | Wait until tomorrow. The limit is 10 a day per manager and per brewery. |
| Your brewery doesn't have an active subscription | Renew on the [Billing](/dashboard/billing) page. You can still remove people while you wait. |
| Can't remove or demote someone | A brewery must always keep one manager. Make someone else a manager first. |

## The dashboard shows only Billing

Your organization does not have an active subscription. Either there has never been one, it was cancelled or not finished, or a payment has been overdue for more than 30 days. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing) to subscribe or renew.

If you have just subscribed, select **Check for my subscription** on the Billing page to link it.

## Device shows Offline or no readings

A device is **Offline** when it has not reported for more than 2 hours, and **Stale** after 10 minutes.

1. Check that the unit has power and that its network connection is working. If power cuts are common on site, a [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup) keeps the FizzWizz running through short outages.
2. Wait 10 minutes. A **Stale** device often recovers on its own.
3. If you recently moved or reinstalled the unit, confirm the setup is complete.
4. Work through [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

## A claimed device is missing, or the claim code doesn't work

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You missed the claim code on the display | Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) with the serial number for a new code |
| *That code isn't right* | Check for a typo and try again |
| *That code has expired* | Codes last 30 days. Wait for the unit to report again and use the new code on its display. |
| *That device already belongs to an organization* | Someone has already claimed it. Contact support with the serial number if it should be yours. |
| *Your subscription covers N device(s)* | Add a device to your subscription in the billing portal, or release one you no longer use |
| A device disappeared from your list | Someone in your organization may have released it. Claim it again with the new code on its display, then set up its alert rules again, because releasing deletes them. |
| A device is missing from the list, but others show | Check the filter buttons and search box above the cards. Select **All**, or **Show all** if nothing matches. |

See [Adding & Claiming a Device](/dashboard/adding-a-device).

## Alerts not firing

**Check in order:**

1. **Plan includes alert rules.** See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).
2. **The rule exists on that device, and its switch is on.** Rules are switched off when a plan stops including alert rules, and do not turn themselves back on.
3. **The reading went past the threshold.** It must be strictly above or below it, in the units you entered the threshold in.
4. **Cooldown.** A rule does not fire again for 30 minutes after it fires.
5. **The device is reporting.** A Stale or Offline device sends no readings to check.
6. **Subscription is active**, or within its 30-day grace period.

For **automatic alerts**, also check that the alert's switch is on under **Automatic alerts** on the Alerts page, that the tank reported in the last 30 minutes, and that the same problem hasn't already alerted in the last 2 hours. **Carbonation stalled** needs a carbonation target on the batch. See [Automatic alerts](/dashboard/alerts-and-rules#automatic-alerts).

## No time-to-target estimate, or a batch isn't marked ready

1. **The batch has a carbonation target.** Without one, there is no estimate and no ready time. See [Setting a carbonation target](/dashboard/batches-and-targets#setting-a-carbonation-target).
2. **Read the message.** **ETA after a few more readings**, **Not rising: no ETA**, **Too unsteady for an ETA** and **Rising slowly** each explain why there is no time yet. See [Time to target and ready](/dashboard/batches-and-targets#time-to-target-and-ready).
3. **Ready takes 2 hours in target.** A batch is marked ready only after its carbonation has stayed inside the target for 2 hours. Changing the target restarts this.
4. **Ready notices are opt-in.** Tick **Also tell me when a batch is ready** on a channel in [Notification Preferences](/dashboard/notification-preferences#batch-ready-notices).

## No Acknowledge button on a push notification

The **Acknowledge** button appears on Android phones and on computers. iPhone and iPad notifications don't show it. Tap the notification to open BrewWizz, then acknowledge the alert on the Alerts page or from the bell.

## Weekly summary email not arriving

1. **It is turned on.** The weekly summary is off until you turn it on yourself under **Weekly summary email** on the Notifications page. It goes out on Mondays, covering the week before.
2. **Your sign-in email hasn't changed.** If it has, the card shows **Paused: your sign-in email changed.** Turn it on again.
3. **You didn't unsubscribe.** Using the link in an earlier email turns it off. Turn it back on from the Notifications page.
4. **Check your spam or junk folder** for the address shown on the card.
5. **Your subscription is active.** The weekly summary stops while the dashboard is locked.

## Alert shows in the dashboard but no notification arrives

1. Open **Notifications** and check that the channel is listed and switched on.
2. A channel marked **Not on plan** is not included in your current plan.
3. For email, check your spam or junk folder.
4. For SMS, see below. For push, see [Push Notifications](/mobile-app/notifications#troubleshooting).

## SMS not received

1. **Plan includes SMS.** The channel is marked **Not on plan** if it doesn't.
2. **Number verified.** A number marked **Not verified** gets no alerts. Select **Text me a code to verify this number**.
3. **Opted out.** If the number ever texted STOP, the switch is locked. Text **START** to BrewWizz Alerts from that phone.
4. **Number format.** Enter numbers in international format, such as `+15551234567`.
5. If all of these are correct, contact BrewWizz support.

| Keyword | Action |
| --- | --- |
| STOP | Unsubscribe from all BrewWizz SMS |
| START | Opt back in |
| HELP | Get help information |

## Upgrade appears in the menu

**Upgrade** replaces **Alerts** and **Notifications** when your plan does not include in-app alerts, or when your organization has no active subscription. **API** is simply hidden when your plan does not include API access. See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

## Values are in the wrong units

Units are a personal setting. Change them with the unit selectors in the header (in **More** on a phone), on the Notifications page, or under **Display units** on a device page.

## Values look out of date

Check **Updated … ago** at the top of the Devices page or device page. The dashboard reloads by itself when you come back to it after a while away, or when your connection returns. If it still looks old, reload the page. If **Last reading** is old too, the device itself has stopped reporting; see [Device shows Offline or no readings](#device-shows-offline-or-no-readings). See [Display units](/dashboard/overview#display-units).

## Buttons are greyed out

If an amber bar reads **You are offline — data may be outdated**, your connection has dropped. Buttons that save changes stay disabled until you are back online.
