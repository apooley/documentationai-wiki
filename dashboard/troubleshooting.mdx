## Common issues and how to fix them

Each section lists the most likely causes first. For problems specific to the installed app on a phone, see [Mobile App Troubleshooting](/mobile-app/troubleshooting).

## Can't sign in

1. Check your email address and password. **Incorrect email or password. Please try again.** means one of them is wrong.
2. If you forgot your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. See [Signing In & Account Setup](/dashboard/signing-in#resetting-your-password).
3. **Too many sign-in attempts** means you need to wait a few minutes before trying again.
4. New user? Open the invitation email from BrewWizz and set your password first. There is no sign-up page. To get an account, email [info@brewwizz.com](mailto:info@brewwizz.com).

## The dashboard shows only Billing

Your organization does not have an active subscription. Either there has never been one, it was cancelled or not finished, or a payment has been overdue for more than 30 days. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing) to subscribe or renew.

If you have just subscribed, select **Check for my subscription** on the Billing page to link it.

## Device shows Offline or no readings

A device is **Offline** when it has not reported for more than 2 hours, and **Stale** after 10 minutes.

1. Check that the unit has power and that its network connection is working. If power cuts are common on site, a [Battery Backup](/setup/battery-backup) keeps the FizzWizz running through short outages.
2. Wait 10 minutes. A **Stale** device often recovers on its own.
3. If you recently moved or reinstalled the unit, confirm the setup is complete.
4. Work through [Device Offline or Not Reporting](/troubleshooting/device-offline).

## A claimed device is missing, or the claim code doesn't work

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You missed the claim code on the display | Contact [Support & Parts](/get-started/support-and-parts) with the serial number for a new code |
| The code is rejected | Check for a typo. The code may have expired (after 30 days), or the device may already belong to an organization. |
| *Your subscription covers N device(s)* | Add a device to your subscription in the billing portal, or release one you no longer use |
| A device disappeared from your list | Someone in your organization may have selected **Release device**. Claim it again with the new code on its display. |

See [Adding & Claiming a Device](/dashboard/adding-a-device).

## Alerts not firing

**Check in order:**

1. **Plan includes alert rules.** See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).
2. **The rule exists on that device, and its switch is on.** If the device was released and claimed again, or moved to your organization, set its rules up again.
3. **The reading went past the threshold.** It must be strictly above or below it, in the units you entered the threshold in.
4. **Cooldown.** A rule does not fire again for 30 minutes after it fires.
5. **The device is reporting.** A Stale or Offline device sends no readings to check.
6. **Subscription is active**, or within its 30-day grace period.

## Alert shows in the dashboard but no notification arrives

1. Open **Notifications** and check that the channel is listed and switched on.
2. A channel marked **Not on plan** is not included in your current plan.
3. For email, check your spam or junk folder.
4. For SMS, see below. For push, see [Push Notifications](/mobile-app/notifications#troubleshooting).

## SMS not received

1. **Plan includes SMS.** The channel is marked **Not on plan** if it doesn't.
2. **Number verified.** A number marked **Not verified** gets no alerts. Select **Text me a code to verify this number**.
3. **Opted out.** If the number ever texted STOP, the switch is locked. Text **START** to BrewWizz Alerts from that phone.
4. **Number format.** Enter numbers in international format, such as `+15551234567`.
5. If all of these are correct, contact BrewWizz support.

| Keyword | Action |
| --- | --- |
| STOP | Unsubscribe from all BrewWizz SMS |
| START | Opt back in |
| HELP | Get help information |

## Upgrade appears in the menu

**Upgrade** replaces a menu item when your plan does not include that feature. See [Understanding Your Plan](/dashboard/understanding-your-plan).

## Values are in the wrong units

Units are a personal setting. Change them with the unit selectors in the header, or under **Display units** on a device page. See [Display units](/dashboard/overview#display-units).

## Buttons are greyed out

If an amber bar reads **You are offline — data may be outdated**, your connection has dropped. Buttons that save changes stay disabled until you are back online.
