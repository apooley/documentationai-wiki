## Not sure where to start?

- **Using a FizzWizz to carbonate a beverage?** Start with [FizzWizz at a Glance](/get-started/fizzwizz-overview), then follow [Setup & Installation](/setup/installation-overview).
- **Using a PacWizz on a filling line?** Read [PacWizz Safety & Site Requirements](/pacwizz/safety), then [Packaging Connections](/pacwizz/packaging-connections).
- **Using a PacWizz to prepare a tank before transfer?** Read [PacWizz Safety & Site Requirements](/pacwizz/safety), then [Purging Connections](/pacwizz/purging-connections).

The **product selector** in the navigation lets you switch between FizzWizz and PacWizz at any time. This page is a standalone platform-level guide — you can return to it anytime from the **Choose Your Product** link in the top navigation. It does not appear inside either product's documentation sections.

<Callout kind="info">

Instructions are specific to each unit. Use the guide that matches the product name on your front panel; do not apply FizzWizz connection or operating instructions to a PacWizz.

</Callout>
